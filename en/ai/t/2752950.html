It is not possible to order a single lock barrel or to order barrels using only the part code. When ordering barrels, observe the special procedure set out in operation 5580 - Special Accessories.

Before working on the Air Bag, it is essential to carry out the following preliminary operations: (1) turn the ignition off and withdraw the key; (2) disconnect the battery terminals and insulate them; (3) wait at least 10 minutes before proceeding.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES (see assembly 5508C - AIR BAG SYSTEM).
