Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The system operates with the ignition key turned ON.

The rearscreen wiper is operated by turning the ring nut at the end of the right steering column switch unit lever.

The operation of the rearscreen wiper motor is controlled intermittently by the body computer node. The rearscreen wiper is activated in synchronization with the operation of the windscreen wiper, but at half the frequency, in all windscreen wiper operating conditions (intermittent/continuous).

The windscreen washer function is operated by pushing the lever gently towards the dashboard: the rearscreen washer pump (the same one as for the windscreen washer) is activated in this way; if the lever is kept in this position, the rearscreen wiper will also come on; When the lever is released, the windscreen wipers will continue to operate for several strokes. After about six seconds, calculated from when the rearscreen wiper park position is reached at the end of the last stroke cycle, there is an additional rearscreen wiper stroke (after-wipe function).

When the windscreen wiper is working, engaging reverse gear automatically activates the rearscreen wiper.

The rearscreen wash/wipe function is managed entirely by the body computer node.
