2127J CHECK ON CORRECT ENGAGEMENT OF ALL GEARS USING THE GEAR LEVER WITH THE ENGINE RUNNING

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Check that, with the engine on and the vehicle stationary: it is possible to engage 1st - 2nd - R gears using the gear lever with the brake pressed.

Check that while moving, all the gears can be engaged depending on the vehicle's speed.

With the vehicle stationary, sometimes repeated engagement attempts (retry) are possible due to mechanical sticking of the gears (especially if the gearbox is new). If the phenomenon continues, it is a symptom of a malfunction
