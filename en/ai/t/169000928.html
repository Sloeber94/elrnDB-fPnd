3350EC LATERAL ACCELERATION/YAW SENSOR CHECK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Check that there is a reference supply of 5 V at pin 1.

Check that there is a power supply of 12 V at pin 3.

Check that there is earth at pin 6.

Check that the sensor output voltage at pins 4 and 5 is between 0.3 and 4.7 V.
