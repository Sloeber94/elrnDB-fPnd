Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean the area of the roof where the moulding will be bonded and remove any previous residues (where fastened to the same door).

1. Position the moulding in its seat and secure with adhesive.

If the moulding removed previously is re-used, clean off any adhesive traces and apply a new adhesive with the same properties.

2. Tighten the retaining nuts.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV

1. Fit the front panel in position.
