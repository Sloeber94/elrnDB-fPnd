Disconnect the electrical connection.

Name

Connector

Air distribution flap actuator

N80

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the air conditioner air distribution flap motor.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Fit the air conditioner air distribution flap motor, checking that the references on the motor sprocket (1a and the distribution flaps sprocket (1b are correctly positioned.

If the distribution flap motor has to be replaced, the new component has to undergo the Proxi initialisation procedure using the diagnostic station (Examiner).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Tighten the fixing bolts.

Connect the electrical connection.
