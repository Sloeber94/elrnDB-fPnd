The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1121 Valve Relays"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error is signalled if the control unit detects an open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the relay or the feedback, if the relay or the operating circuit is damaged

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the electrohydraulic control unit connector is not faulty.

Check that the valve relay connector is not faulty at the pins (on both the wiring side and the control unit side) by carrying out a visual inspection.

In particular, check that:

- They are not oxidized

- They are not distorted or broken

Check the state of the power fuse in the engine compartment junction unit

See E7020 ABS
