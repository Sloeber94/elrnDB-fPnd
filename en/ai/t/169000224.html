The Connect screen does not come on (goes on and off sporadically)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Withdraw the Connect unit from the facia and check the connections

Check the condition of the fuse on the Connect body.

Check that the correct power supplies are present at the Connect connectors

See Test 5580PO CONNECT POWER SUPPLY CHECK+C141

Move on to Step 2

  • Fuse blown
  • No power supply
  • Incorrect earth connection
  • Renew the blown fuse after ascertaining the cause
  • Restore correct power supplySee E4090 NAVIGATOR
  • Restore the correct earth connectionSee E4090 NAVIGATOR

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that it is working properly

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace Connect
