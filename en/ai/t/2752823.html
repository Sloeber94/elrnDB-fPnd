Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The rear wheel alignment should be checked after the components which affect the wheel geometry have been subjected to the following checks:

tyre inflation pressure;

Measurement

Value

-

Front wheel pressure (bar)

2

Measurement

Value

-

Rear wheel pressure (bar)

1.9

The eccentricity and the orthogonality of the wheel rims should not exceed 3 mm;

wheel bearing end float;

Carry out the preliminary checks on the vehicle and remove any loads transported.

Position the vehicle (in running order) on a lift with test equipment.

Check the alignment of the rear wheels.

The rear suspension consists of a rigid axle and therefore the wheel alignment cannot be altered. If the rear wheels are not aligned, check the drive axle and the bodyshell.
