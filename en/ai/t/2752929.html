5505A90 rear wiring bundle - r r or replace

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before working on the Air Bag system, it is essential to carry out the following preliminary operations: (1) turn the ignition off and remove the key; (2) disconnect the battery terminals and insulate them; (3) wait 10 minutes before proceeding.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES (see assembly 5508C - AIR BAG SYSTEM).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
