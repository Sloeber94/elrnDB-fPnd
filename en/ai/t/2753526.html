Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The supply for the headlamp alignment correction motors, integrated in the front light clusters F010 and F011, is provided by the body computer node M001, from pin 6 connector B. The supply is only provided if the dipped headlamps are switched on (for further details See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS ).

The front light cluster parabola position setting is managed by the switches in the instrument panel E050. The instrument panel E050 receives the earth reference C020 connector B and the ignition-operated supply at pin 2 and 7 connector A, respectively.

Earth C020 is fastened to the passenger compartment floor and is located near the right front wheel arch (footwell area). Coupling D001 is housed in the lower part of the right front pillar, near the door cable duct.

The instrument panel sends the signal to the electric motors for correcting the headlamp alignment from pin 6 connector A.

The instrument panel shows the headlamp aglinment correction setting through an LCD.
