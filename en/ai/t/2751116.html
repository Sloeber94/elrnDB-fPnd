Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the relevant control is operated, the radio comes on but it cannot be heard properly or crackles, radio station reception is disturbed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Activate the self-diagnostic procedure and check that the AM/FM aerial is connected correctly when on SETUP screen 5.

See Test 5580PJ CONNECT SELF-DIAGNOSIS PROCEDURE

Move on to Step 1

  • Aerial connection defective
  • Aerial impedence defective or interrupted
  • Repair the connection between aerial and Connect
  • Replace the aerialOp. 5570T61 MULTI-FUNCTION AERIAL - R R

1

RECEPTION AREA CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is not present in areas of poor reception. The reception should improve significantly when the vehicle moves away from such areas. If the reception area is very poorly served, attempts to switch between frequencies may be perceived as pauses

Move on to Step 2

Area with poor reception

Move to an area with the radio reception is good and try FM and non-FM stations

2
