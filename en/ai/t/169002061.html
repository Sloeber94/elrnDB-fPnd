L015 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT HEATING

Path
Fault diagnosis > L CLIMATE CONTROL > L015 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT HEATING
Kind
fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fPnd/en/#/169002061

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

The climate control is working properly and sends air according to the preset flows but this air does not guarantee the required heating.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the engine coolant level is within the preset levels and that the refrigerant fluid circulates correctly in the system

Move on to Step 1

Engine coolant level too low.

Restore the correct level checking that there are no leaks from the system.

Refrigerant fluid not circulating correctly (thermostatic valve does not open sufficiently)

See fault diagnosis for thermostatic valve

CHECK ON CORRECT CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

With the engine on, the compressor off, in the manual function with the temperature knob at the maximum hot position. Select the recirculation button the the closed position and set the fan speed to 3 and front ventilation distribution. Using the diagnostic equipment check that the engine temperature reaches at least 70 °C

The temperature of the air coming out of the vents is not the desired one

Move on to Step 2
