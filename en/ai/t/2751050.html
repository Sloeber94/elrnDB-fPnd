With the ignition ON, when the steering column switch unit lever is raised or lowered, the direction indicators do not work. The hazard warning lights do not work when the button is pressed

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Press the hazard warning lights switch in the control panel and check that the direction indicators/hazard warning lights are activated and deactivated

With the ignition ON, using the special lever, check that the left/right direction indicators are activated

End of diagnosis

DIRECTION INDICATORS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

Operation
