Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Check that the sensor output voltage at pin 2 is between 0.3 and 4.7 V (pins 4 and 5).

- Check that the power supply at pin 3 is between 4.7 and 5.3 V.

- Check that there is earth at pin 1.

Longitudinal acceleration sensor pin out

Pin 1 - Earth

Pin 2 - Signal

Pin 3 - 5 V power supply
