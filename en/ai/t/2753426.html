2127D OIL PRESSURE CHECK

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

Connect the Examiner Plus or Smart diagnostic equipment and take a pressure reading.

With the pump switched off, the pressure should be between 40.5 and 51 bar at ambient temperature.
