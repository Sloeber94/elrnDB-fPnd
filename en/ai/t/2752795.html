Refitting

Removing

Refit the boot onto the rear shock absorber

Follow these instructions for replacing the shock absorbers.If the mileage for the shock absorbers is more than 20,000 - 25,000 Km, always replace both shock absorbers for each axle together, taking care to fit the same type of shock absorbers from those available as spares on the same axle.If the mileage for the shock absorbers is below 20,000 - 25,000 Km, then one shock absorber only can be replaced, as long as the new one is the same type and after having checked that the other shock absorber is in perfect working order.

Before refitting the front shock absorber components, check that there are no signs of cracks or distortions in the spring which would adversely affect its operation. Also check the integrity of the boot. If there are surface cuts or cracks replace the boot itself

If the springs are being replaced, ensure that the colour of the paint marks on the outside of the central coils matches.Versions with mechanical steering have brown paint marks.Versions with electric steering with a heating system and petrol engines have white paint marks.Versions with electric steering with air conditioning and petrol engines have blue paint marks.Versions with JTD engines have pink paint marks.
