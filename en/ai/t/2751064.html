Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the key in the ON position, when the lever located on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the windscreen wipers do not work properly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is pulled towards it, the windscreen washer works properly.

Continue to Step 1

The windscreen washer does not work

Coolant leaks detected

See table J01 WINDSCREEN/REARSCREEN WASHERS NOT WORKING

1

CHECK ON REAR WINDOW WIPER MOTOR SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • motor connection to earth;
  • motor suply line;
  • connections with the Body Computer See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Continue to Step 2

Connection broken

Restore the connections See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner Plus or Smart) to the diagnostic socket and check that there are no faults in the Body Computer

Continue to Step 3

Body Computer faulty

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

3

CHECK ON WINDSCREEN WIPER MOTOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check correct operation of windscreen wiper motor

Continue to Step 4

faulty windscreen wiper motor

Replace the windscreen wiper motor Op. 5050B42 windscreen wiper motor - r+r

4

CHECK ON STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly, in particular the contacts with the windscreen wiper motor

Continue to Step 5

Faulty steering column switch unit

Replace the stalk unit Op. 5050A12

5

Continue as instructed by the tester

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
