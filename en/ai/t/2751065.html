Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the lever on the right of the steering wheel is pulled towards the latter, the windscreen washer function does not work properly

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Carry out a preliminary check on the fluid level in the reservoir; also make sure that it is not leaking.

Check that when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the windscreen wipers work properly.

Check that the jets are not obstructed by ice or dirt

Continue to Step 1

Mixture level too low

Top up the level Op. 5050A14

Mixture level too low

Replace the reservoir Op. 5050A10 windscreen washer fluid reservoir (in engine bay) - r+r

Mixture level too low

Coolant leaks detected

See table J01 WINDSCREEN/REARSCREEN WASHERS NOT WORKING

1

ELECTRIC WINDSCREEN WASHER PUMP SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the connection between the steering column switch unit, Body Computer, junction unit and electric windscreen washer pump See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Continue to Step 2

Connection broken

Restore the windscreen washer connections See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner Plus or Smart) to the diagnostic socket and check that there are no faults in the Body Computer

Continue to Step 3

Body Computer faulty

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

3

ELECTRIC WINDSCREEN WASHER PUMP CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the operation of the electric windscreen washer pump

Continue to Step 4

Electric pump faulty

Replace the electric windscreen washer pump Op. 5050B22 front and rear screen washer pump - r+r

4

CHECK ON STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly

Continue to Step 5

Steering column switch unit not working

Replace the steering column switch unit Op. 5050A12

5

Continue as instructed by the tester

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
