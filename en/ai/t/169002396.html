Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Operational Description

The reversing lights switch:

  • at pin 1 receives an ignition-operated supply from the line protected by fuse F31;
  • at pin 2, when reverse gear is engaged, supplies the signal to the body computer node M001, at pin 1 connector C.

The body computer node, receives the signal:

  • at pin 1 connector F supplies the reversing light, housed inside the left rear light cluster F030;
  • at pin 6 connector F, sends the signal to the parking sensor control unit (where fitted) M084See E3090 PARKING SENSOR

The left rear light cluster F030 has a reference earth C030, secured to the left rear wheel arch (passenger compartment interior).
