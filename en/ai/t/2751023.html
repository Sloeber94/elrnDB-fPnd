PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • the condition of the air intake circuit
  • that there are no faults in the air filter
  • whether the diesel pipes are leaking
  • that the battery earth is correctly fixed
  • that there is no water, petrol, paraffin or dirt in the fuel tank and filter
  • that there no obstructions in the exhaust system

Continue to Step 1

Fault in air intake circuit

Repair the air intake circuit

Air filter blocked

Replace the filter element

Leak from diesel pipes

Eliminate the leaks

Loose battery earth terminal

Restore the correct fixing of the earth cable

Presence of water, petrol, paraffin or dirt detected

Clean the tank and pipes and replace the filter element

Obstruction in the exhaust system

Replace the damaged system component

  • absence of leaks in high pressure circuit

Diesel pressure parameter incorrect

INJECTOR HIGH PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, as described in the test See Test 1060C Injector high pressure fuel circuit check, that diesel leaks from the injector recirculation connections are small and about the same as one another.

Replace the injector
