Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diagnostic equipment signals one or more of the following errors: "C1040 Right front speed sensor, electrical error", "C1035 Left front speed sensor, electrical error", "C1050 Right rear speed sensor, electrical error" and/or "C1045 Left rear speed sensor, electrical error"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error is signalled if the control unit detects an open circuit or short circuit (positive or to earth) at one of the sensors or the wiring. This fault can also be detected if the control unit connector is damaged
