Removing

Refitting

Remove the car from the ramps.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and disconnect the air supply hose between the turbocharger and the heat exchanger (1b).

2. Remove the gasket.

3. Release the air supply hose between the turbocharger and the heat exchangger (3b).

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

2. Release the air supply hose from the heat exchanger and the turbocharger and remove it.
