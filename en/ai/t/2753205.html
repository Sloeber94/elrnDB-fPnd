If the car is fitted with an Air Bag system, before working on it, it is essential to carry out the following preliminary operations: (1) turn the ignition off and remove the key; (2) disconnect the battery termnals and insulate them; (3) wait at least 10 minutes before proceeding.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY FULES ( 5580C air bag system ).
