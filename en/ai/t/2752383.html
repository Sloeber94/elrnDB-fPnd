Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

This procedure is carried out if the hydraulic circuit pipes or components have been fitted/refitted.

The brake pedal must be pressed for the entire duration of the operation.

Connect the Examiner test instrument and carry out the operations indicated in the specific procedure for bleeding air from the hydraulic circuit of the electrohydraulic gear change system.
