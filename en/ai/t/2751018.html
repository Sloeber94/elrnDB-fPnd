ENGAGEMENT/SELECTION SENSORS REPLACEMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

If there is a gear engagement sensor error and no faults are detected at the connections/wiring, replace it Op. 2127E42 hydraulic selection gearbox shift position sensor - r rand check that the position reading is correct by carrying out a series of C.M.

If there is a gear selector sensor error and no faults are detected at the connections/wiring, replace it Op. 2127E40 hydraulic selection gearbox speed selection position sensor - r rand check that the position reading is correct by carrying out a series of C.M.

It is advisable to mark the two potentiometers in order to be able to recognize them once they have been removed
