Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner and Smart gearbox and ESP

Functional description

The engine control unit M010 receives signals from the coolant temperature sensor K036 and the linear pressure sensor K120.

In accordance with these data, the engine control unit M010 activates the first or second speed of the fan N011.

Activation of first speed:

  • from pin 55 connector B, the engine control unit, via a negative signal, energises the relay T06;
  • the direct battery supply supplied by the relay T06 passes through the resistor O010 and reaches the fan N011.

Activation of second speed:

  • from pin 59, connector B, the engine control unit, via a negative signal, energises the relay T07;
  • the direct battery supply provided by the relay T07 reaches the fan N011 directly.

The engine management control unit M010 sends engine coolant temperature information, via the C-CAN See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES , to the body computer mode M001 (pin 11, 12, connector D). The body computer node sends this information, via the B-CAN, to the instrument panel E050, for the management of the temperature gauge inside the actual panel (for more details See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL ).
