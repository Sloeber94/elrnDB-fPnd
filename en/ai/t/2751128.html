There is a noise whilst driving from the area of the sun roof;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

FINISHING TRIM CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

(With the roof closed) check that the front and rear finishing trims between the sun roof and the bodyshell are correctly fitted and the moulding profile is correct

Continue to Step 2

Fitting/matching not correct

Restore correct fitting/matching

2

FRONT SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the sun roof housing front seal is correctly fitted and is not damaged

Continue to Step 3

Seal faulty

Restore correct operation

3

ADJUSTMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

(With the roof closed) check that the front moving glass and the rear fixed glass are correctly matched (the rear profile of the front moving glass should be higher than the front profile of the rear fixed glass)

Continue to Step 4

Coupling incorrect

Restore correct fitting/matching

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
