5550DA BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH OPERATION CHECK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- The brake switch has four pins.

- Connect to the first two using a multimeter and check, by pressing the switch, that the circuits switches from normally closed to normally open.

- Connect the probes to the other two and check, by pressing the switch, that the circuits switches from normally open to normally closed.
