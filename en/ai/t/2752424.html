Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Raise the vehicle.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Undo the plug and drain the engine oil into a suitable container.

Keep the metal gasket underneath.

The engine oil used contains substances that are harmful to the environment. These substances should be placed in specially designed containers.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods
