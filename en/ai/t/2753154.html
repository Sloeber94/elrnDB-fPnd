If the car is fitted with an Air Bag system, before working on it, it is essential to carry out the following preliminary operations: (1) turn the ignition off and remove the key; (2) disconnect the battery termnals and insulate them; (3) wait at least 10 minutes before proceeding.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY FULES ( 5580C air bag system ).

Connect the battery terminals.

After the battery has been disconnected or a fuse has blown, the system must be initialized again, proceeding as follows:

  • press the switch in the 'roof closed' position;
  • keep the switch pressed: the roof will close in stages;
  • keeping the switch pressed, wait for the roof to close fully until the motor is stopped;
  • release the switch.
