Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The reversing light switch:

  • pin 1 receives an ignition-operated power supply from a line protected by fuse F31;
  • pin 2 supplies a signal to body computer node M001 pin 1 connector C when reverse is engaged.

The body computer, after receiving the signal:

  • pin 1 connector F supplies the reversing light housed inside right tail-light F031;
  • pin 6 connector F sends a signal to parking sensor control unit (where present) M084 See E3090 PARKING SENSOR .

right tail-light F031 refers to earth C030 secured to the left rear tail-light (inside passenger compartment).
