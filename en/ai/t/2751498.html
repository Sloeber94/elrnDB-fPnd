Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a front cigar lighter/current socket located in the centre console.

Its operation is controlled by a specific relay and it is protected by a fuse, both located in the engine compartment junction unit. The direct battery supply is only enabled with the ignition key in the MAR position (INT/A). This strategy is designed to provide the maximum current to the engine whilst it is switched on, given the high absorption of any consumers connected to the socket.

The cigar lighter/current socket has its own internal lighting which is activated when the side lights are on.

To operate the cigar lighter, simply press the knob: after a few seconds it pops out automatically, ready for use.
