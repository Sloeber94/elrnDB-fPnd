A leak of engine coolant is seen with dripping and/or fouling of the engine

Versions with and without air conditioning.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operation

Carry out a visual inspect to locate the component or area which is leaking.

If the visual inspection is not sufficient, wash the engine, dry it, start it and run it slowly until it comes up to temperature; to identify the leak, carry out a leak test on the system ( Op. 0510C30 cooling system - tightness test)
