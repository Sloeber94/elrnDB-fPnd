Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diagnostic equipment signals one or more of the following errors: "C1141 Primary Valve 1 (VLV1)", "C1151 Primary Valve 2 (VLV2)", "C1146 Pilot Valve 1 (USV1)" and/or "C1156 Pilot Valve 2 (USV2)"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error is signalled if the ASR solenoid valves cannot be activated. This means that there is an open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the solenoid valves or the solenoid valves connection or that the operating circuit is damaged

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
