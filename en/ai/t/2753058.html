Removing

Refitting

Lower the window completely.

1. Position a strip of suitable adhesive tape along the bottom edge of the outer weather strip.

2. Using the special tool, remove the outer weather strip.

Name

Country

-

Scraper blade

1.878.077.000

Be very careful not to damage the door or the paintwork.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Remove the strip of adhesive tape fitted previously.

Clean the sealant residues from the door.

Check the condition of the outer weather strip.

Fit the outer weather strip in its seating.

Check that the outer weather strip is working properly.
