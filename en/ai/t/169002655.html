Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

Description

The engine control unit consists of a fan operated by the engine management unit in accordance with parameters supplied by the coolant sensor.

Fan operation is governed by a dedicated relay located in the engine bay junction unit.l

The supply line for the fan and the relay energizing circuit are protected by a special fuse located in the engine compartment junction unit.
