Results of the tests

1

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the radio and check that the telephone connector is correctly inserted in its housing

Continue to Step 2

Connector disconnected

Insert the connector in its housing

2

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the following at the telephone connector:

  • the power supply
  • connections with the radio See E3580 PREPARATION FOR CELLULAR TELEPHONE

Continue to Step 3

Fault in positive supply and/or earth

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections with the radio faulty

Restore the connections

3

AERIAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the coaxial aerial cable and the actual aerial - check aerial amplifier supply See E3580 PREPARATION FOR CELLULAR TELEPHONE

Continue to Step 4

Aerial cable faulty

Renew/replace the aerial lead

Amplifier supply leads faulty

Renew/replace the supply lead

Amplifier supply leads faulty

Replace the amplifier and/or aerial

4

Continue as instructed by the tester

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the telephone support
