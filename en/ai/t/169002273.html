Thoroughly clean the area of the wheelarch where the seal will be bonded and remove any previous residues (where fastened to the same door).

1. Position the seal in its seat and secure with adhesive.

If the seal removed previously is re-used, clean off any adhesive traces and apply a new adhesive with the same properties.
