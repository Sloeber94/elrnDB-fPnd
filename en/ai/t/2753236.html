Refitting

7045D31 Front seat BACKREST COVER, left or right - R.R. with seat removed

1. Release the hooks securing the lumbar suspension to the frame.

2. Release the hooks fastening the two transmission cables for the lumbar device from the backrest frame.

3. Undo the bolts fixing the lumbar control device to the backrest frame.

4. Remove the lumbar adjustment device from the backrest frame.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check the condition of the backrest lumbar manual adjustment device.

Check the condition of the two transmission cables and the lumbar suspension.

Fit the lumbar adjustment device in the backrest frame.

Secure the lumbar suspension hooks to the backrest frame.

Secure the hooks fastening the transmission cables to the backrest frame.

Tighten the two bolts fixing the lumbar adjustment device to the backrest frame.
