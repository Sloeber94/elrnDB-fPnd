0010T64 brake and or clutch fluid check level and top up as necessary

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Check that the brake fluid in the reservoir is at the maximum level; if this is not the case, top up wth the recommended fluid; If the brake fluid level is being topped up, remove the cap and introduce fluid until the maximum level is reached.

Type

Component

Name

Qty.

-

Synthetic fluid

BRAKE FLUID

TUTELA TOP 4

-

The symbol on the container identifies synthetic type brake fluids as opposed to mineral type brake fluids. The use of mineral type fluids irreparably damages the special rubber seals in the hydraulic braking system.

Take great care to avoid the brake fluid, which is highly corrosive, from coming into contact with the paintwork. If this should happen, wash immediately with water.
