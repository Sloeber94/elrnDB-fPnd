The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1161 Brake Light Switch"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error can be signalled in two sets of cases. The first is when there is an open circuit in the brake lights switch or the wiring, the brake lights are burnt out or the reading circuit is damaged.The second set of circumstances is if there is an open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the brake lights switch or wiring, if the switch or the reading circuit is damaged. In all these cases the braking action is detected without pressing the brake pedal switch (or the closure of the switch contacts)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

BRAKE STATUS PEDAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment and, in the "PARAMETERS" ambient, check the "BRAKE PEDAL STATUS".

Press the brake pedal and read "PRESSED".

Release the brake pedal and read "RELEASED"

Move on to Step 5

Incorrect reading

Move on to Step 3

4

BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH ADJUSTMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Adjust the brake pedal switch so that when the pedal is completely depressed it does not reach the last notch, with reference to Service News 3350 E 000 AA N°33 03 02

Move on to Step 5

to ensure that the control unit checks whether there are any errors still present.
