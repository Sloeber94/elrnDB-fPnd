Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

Description

The engine cooling system consists of an electric fan, operated by the engine control unit, in accordance with the parameters supplied by the coolant temperature sensor.

The operation of the fan is controlled by a specific relay, located in the engine compartment junction unit.

The fan supply line and the relay energisation circuit are protected by a specific fuse located in the engine compartment junction unit.
