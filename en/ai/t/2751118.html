PRELIMINARY CHECK

Operation

Check that the fault does not stem from incorrect use.

Check that the buttons are working on versions with controls on the steering wheel.

Continue to Step 1

CD not correctly positioned

Restore the correct position.

Faulty CD holder

Restore the operation of the CD holder.

Controls on the steering wheel faulty

See table N70 CONTROLS ON THE STEERING WHEEL NOT WORKING

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the CD-Changer from its housing. Check for a positive supply at the connector coming from the radio and an earth at the CD-Changer See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 2

No supply (battery positive) and/or earth at the CD-Changer faulty

Continue to Step 3

2

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the CD-Changer removed from its housing. Check for signals, at the radio connector, enabling the reading of the music discs in the CD Changer. See E3510 RADIO See Test 5580PH Check operation of CD- Changer module and radio module

Continue to Step 4

No signals enabling reading of music discs in the CD-Changer.

Continue to Step 3

3

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the following in order at the disconnected CD-Changer connector:

  • Continuity/insulation of the wiring between the radio and the connector for the CD-Changer.
  • Presence of signals enabling reading of music discs See E3510 RADIO See Test 5580PH Check operation of CD- Changer module and radio module

Continue to Step 6

Radio faulty

Replace the radio Op. 5570T80 car radio unit - r+r

Wiring between CD Changer and radio faulty.

Renew the electrical connection. See E3510 RADIO

CD Changer faulty.

Replace the CD-Changer Op. 5570T84

4

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the radio removed from its housing. Check for audio signals, at the radio connector, coming from the CD Changer. See Test 5580PH Check operation of CD- Changer module and radio module

Continue to Step 6

No audio signals from the CD Changer.

Continue to Step 5

5

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the following, in order, with the CD-Changer disconnected:

  • Continuity/insulation of the wiring between the radio and the Cd Changer.
  • presence of music disc audio signals See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 6

CD Changer faulty.

Replace the CD-Changer Op. 5570T85

Wiring between CD Changer and radio faulty.

Renew the electrical connections See E3510 RADIO
