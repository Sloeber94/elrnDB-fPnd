Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV

Description

The vehicle brake lights are located at the rear in the side light clusters and in the special central third brake light.

All the bulbs are operated each time the brake pedal is pressed via a switch on the actual pedal.

The circuit is supplied with the key ON and is protected by a specific fuse located in the junction unit under the dashboard.

The braking signal is also taken from the ABS.
