Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner Plus or Smart) and carry out active diagnosis 'Zeroing phonic wheel initialisation'.

Bring the engine up to temperature (temperature greater than 80° Χ) from minimum level.

Accelerate to 5000 rpm (constant) for about 2-3 seconds and release. Carry out this operation for three consecutive times.

With the ignition off, wait 45 s before re-starting the engine (Power Latch function).
