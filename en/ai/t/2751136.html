CHECKING CYLINDER HEAD

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Remove the cylinder head

That that the following are absent:

  • Wear on valve stem with excessive clearance between the valve stem and valve guide and between the valve stem and oil seals
  • oily deposits on the valves;
  • defects in the cylinder block/crankcase surface;
  • defects in the cylinder head gasket;

Continue to Step 2

Excessive clearance between the valve stem and valve guide and between the valve stem and oil seal

Replace the damaged/worn components

Presence of oily dross on the valves

Overhaul the cylinder head Op. 1016E10 single cylinder head, removed - overhaul

Cylinder block/crankcase plane defective

Reface the cylinder block/crankcase

Cylinder head gasket fault

Check planarity of Cylinder head/Crankcase mating surfaces and replace the cylinder head gasket

CYLINDER LINER/BORE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the cylinder liners/bores. In particular:

  • absence of scoring/vitrification
  • Linear dimensions, particularly the lack of ovality/taper/wear
  • Correct piston - liner installation clearance

Continue to Step 4

Liners with scoring and/or vitrification

Grind the cylinder liners/bores

Dimensions out of tolerance: presence of ovality/taper/wear

Grind the cylinder liners/bores

Fitting clearance incorrect

Replace the pistons, pins and seals, respecting the fitting clearances
