Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The interior lights do not work when the relevant controls are operated (including the boot light).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • condition of individual bulbs
  • condition of the relevant bulb holders
  • state and operation of courtesy light switches

Continue to Step 2

Front courtesy light bulb faulty

Change the bulb Op. 7040E80 COURTESY LIGHT BULB - R.R.

Front courtesy light bulb faulty

Restore or replace the courtesy light Op. 7040E10 front courtesy light - r+r

Front courtesy light bulb faulty

Restore or replace the bulb holder Op. 7040G40

2

CHECK ON INTERIOR LIGHTING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Body Computer supply;
  • connections between courtesy lights and Body Computer;
  • connection to +30 courtesy lights;
  • courtesy light earth connection See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

Continue to Step 3

Connections not correct

Restore the correct connections See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

Connections not correct

Restore the earth connection See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

Connections not correct

Restore connection See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

3

CHECK ON SIGNAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that when the driver's front door or the boot is opened, an earth signal is sent from the Body Computer to the courtesy light

Continue to Step 4

No earth signal from Body Computer

Restore the correct supply to the Body Computer. If necessary, replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

3

CHECK ON SIGNAL

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that when one of the side doors or the boot is opened, an earth signal is sent from the Body Computer to the courtesy light

Continue to Step 4

No earth signal from Body Computer

Restore the correct supply to the Body Computer. If necessary, replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
