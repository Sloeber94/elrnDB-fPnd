1. Release the pipe from the clips.

2. Remove cilp (2a), unscrew connector (2b) joining it to the flexible section, then remove the left front brake rigid pipe (2c) from the engine compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Working in the wheel compartment, fit the rigid pipe from the ABS control unit to the left front brake.

Working in the left front wheel compartment, tighten the connector for the rigid pipe to the flexible section of the left front brake pipe to the recommended torque.
