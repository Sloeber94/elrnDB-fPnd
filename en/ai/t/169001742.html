Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Raise the vehicle.

Op. 4450B08 RIGHT REAR WHEEL R R

[Left or right]

1. Undo the fixing bolt (1a) and raise the caliper (1b).

2. Remove the two brake pads

Removing

Tighten the fixing bolt to torque.

[Left or right]
