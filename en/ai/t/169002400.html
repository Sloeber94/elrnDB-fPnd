Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a reversing light located in the right rear light cluster.

This is operated whenever reverse is engaged via a switch fastened directly to the gear housing.

The circuit is supplied with the ignition ON and protected by a fuse located in the underfacia junction unit.
