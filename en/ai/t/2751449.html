Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The front ceiling light is the rocker type and contains a courtesy light.

The courtesy light is switched on/off according to the following logic:

  • to switch the courtesy light on permanently, press the light on the right hand side;
  • to switch the courtesy light off, press the light on the left hand side;
  • to switch the courtesy light on automatically, keep it in the centre position.

The courtesy light is switched on automatically in the following circumstances:

  • when the driver's door is opened;
  • when the inertia switch is operated, for a maximum of 15 minutes.

The courtesy light is switched off automatically in the following circumstances:

  • when the driver's door is closed;
  • when the inertia switch is reactivated.

The rear courtesy light comes on or goes off when the tailgate is opened or closed, respectively.
