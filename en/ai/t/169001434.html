Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: 4x4

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Raise the vehicle.

Unscrew the rear differential casing filler plug.

Unscrew the rear differential casing drain plug and collect the fluid in a suitable container.

Do not let the oil disperse into the atmosphere as this could cause pollution.

Clean the drain plug.

Screw in the drain plug and tighten it to the recommended torque.

Add fluid to the rear differential casing through the filler hole.

Clean the filler plug.

Tighten the filler plug to the specified torque.

Lower the vehicle and remove from the lift.
