Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is fitted with an extra bright rear fog lamp, located in the left rear light cluster which can be used in all poor visibility conditions.

The rear fog lamps are activated by a button on the control panel;

The rear fog lamps are enabled when the side lights and dipped beams are on, or when the high beams and fog lights are on.

A warning light in the instrument panel signals that the rear foglamps have been switched on.

The body computer node

  • manages rear foglamp activation;
  • monitors the system to ensure it is working efficiently and indicates any faults by turningon a lighting failure warning light on the instrument panel.
