Before removing and refitting the Side Bag sensor, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) turn the ignition off 'STOP' and remove the key; (2) disconnect the battery terminals and insulate them; (3) wait at least 10 minutes before proceeding.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES (see assembly 5508C - AIR BAG SYSTEM).
