Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the engine coolant temperature gauge in the instrument panel is not working;

The engine coolant temperature value message and the engine temperature sensor failure signal is made available by the Engine Management Node on the C-CAN; the Body Computer Node makes the message available to the B-CAN and consequently to the instrument panel
