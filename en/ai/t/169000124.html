PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Do not carry out the checks with the customer's SIM card. Carry out the test using a dedicated SIM card that is proved to have been working on other vehicles

Check the intensity of the range received reading the number of bars on the display

Check the electrical connection of the GSM aerial lead

GSM aerial check

See Test 5580PK GSM ANTENNA OPERATION CHECK

Move on to Step 1

  • Poor reception in the area through lack of radio signal cover
  • GSM aerial lead connection at Connect defective
  • GSM aerial defective
  • Move to an area covered by the radio signal
  • Renew the connectionSee E3570 INTEGRATED RADIO PHONE
  • Replace the GSM aerialOp. 5570T61 MULTI-FUNCTION AERIAL - R R

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the speaker connections are correctly connected

If the customer complains of no or poor audio quality, check:

That the microphone for the hands-free function is working properly

Check that there are no microphone vibrations

End of diagnosis

  • Speaker connections broken
  • microphone not correctly installed
  • Restore continuity
  • Install the microphone correctly
