2. Remove the hydraulic tappets.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Lubricate with engine oil and refit the hydraulic tappets in their seatings.

Position the hydraulic tappets with the oil passage hole facing towards the outside of the bottom cylinder head: this position puts this hole in communication with the oil channel present in the bottom cylinder head.

Place the valve rocker arms back in their housings.
