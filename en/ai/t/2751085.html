PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Move the ring nut and the steering column switch unit lever and check that the parking lights come on correctly.

Press the hazard warning lights switch and check that they are correctly activated.

Then turn the ignition key to the ON position and check that the side/no. plate lights are working.

Activate the rear fog lamp using the control panel and check that it is working properly.

Operate the direction indicators using the stalk lever and check that they are working properly

Press the brake pedal and check that the brake lights come on
