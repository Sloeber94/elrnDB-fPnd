L025 AIR PREHEATING IS NOT ACTIVATED WITH LOW EXTERNAL TEMPERATURE

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

The climate control is working properly and sends air according to the preset flows but this air does not guarantee the required heating, especially if the outside temperature is low

Operation

  • at the climate control node at the CAN line
  • at the engine temperature sensor
  • at the passenger compartment temperature sensor
  • at the mixture actuator

2

CHECK ON CORRECT CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

With the engine on and the compressor off, place the control unit to the AUTO function with the temperature knob at the maximum hot HI position. Also select the recirculation button to the closed position and set the fan speed to 3-4 bars (display) and the distribution to front ventilation. Using the diagnostic equipment check that the engine temperature reaches at least 70°C. There should be a variation in the temperature (getting hotter)

End of diagnosis

The temperature does not appear to get hotter

Move on to Step 3

3

CHECK ON CLIMATE CONTROL NODE ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the electrical connections at the outside temperature sensor, the passenger compartment temperature sensor and the mixture actuator

Move on to Step 4

Electrical connections loose

Renew the damaged electrical connections

Outside temperature sensor not working

Replace the outside temperature sensor

Op. 5010D12 OUTSIDE AIR TEMPERATURE SENSOR ON DOOR MIRROR - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

Passenger compartment sensors not working

Replace the passenger compartment temperature sensors

4

AIR MIXTURE ACTUATOR FLAP CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the air mixture actuator flap completes its travel without any hinderance

End of diagnosis

Flap obstructed or damaged

Remove the air mixture actuator flap impediment or replace the flap, if damaged.

Flap not obstructed

Replace the actuator
