Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

The foglamp activation control is issued by a key inside control panel H090.

Body computer node M001 receive a negative signal ( refers to earth C020 connector B) at pin11 connector O.

Once it has received the signal, it turns on the foglamps:

  • from pin 16 connector D it sends a negative signal to the coil of relay T14 supplied by an ignition-operated supply from a line protected by fuse F31;
  • the relay closes its internal contact and sends a direct battery from pin 71 connettore D of B001 to foglamps F015 and F016.

only if pin 7 connector P receives a side light activation signal E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS - Description .

Earth C020 is secured to the passenger compartment floor and positioned near the front right wheelarch (footwell)

The body computer node communicates from pins 35, 36 of connector I via the CAN network with instrument panel E050 (for further details see wiring diagram E1050) and turns on the foglamp warning light.
