Removing

Refitting

1. Use tool (1a) to prevent the pulley turning, then unscrew retaining bolt (1b).

Name

Country

1a

Counter-torque

1.860.831.001

2. Remove the toothed driven pulley.

3. Remove the front oil seal from its housing.

Removing the oil seal destroys it so a new one must be fitted.
