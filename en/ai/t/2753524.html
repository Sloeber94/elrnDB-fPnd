Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Functional description

the sun roof electric motor includes:

  • the control unit M095;
  • the roof opening/closing switch.

The sun roof control unit receives an ignition-controlled supply (pin 1) from the line protected by fuse F53 and a direct battery supply (pin 2) from the line protected by fuse F46. Both fuses are housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B002.

Depending on the command given, the control unit M095 proceeds directly with opening or closing the moving glass panel for the roof.

Earth C020 is fastened to the passenger compartment floor and is located near the right front wheel arch (footwell area). Coupling D001 is housed in the lower part of the right front pillar, near the door cable duct.
