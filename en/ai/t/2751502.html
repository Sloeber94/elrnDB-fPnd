Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a device that makes it possible to adjust the the headlight beam, depending on the load, directly from the driver's seat. The headlamps are thus always aligned quickly and correctly.

The adjustment device consists of a motor mounted on each of the two headlamps which tilts them as appropriate, lowering the light beam if the car is heavily laden, and raising it if the load decreases.

A special control in the instrument panel makes it possible to select four possible positions.

The upper switch decreases the value of the position (the light beam is raised), the lower switch increases the value of the position (the light beam is lowered). The switch should be pressed for 200 ms. If the switch is pressed for longer, there are no increases or decreases; the switches should be released and pressed again to change position.

The device is only enabled when the dipped headlamps are switched on.
