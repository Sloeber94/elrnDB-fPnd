Description

The vehicle has two special lamps for the dipped beam headlamps.

The dipped beam headlamps are switched on through the steering column switch unit by turning the special ring nut in the position after the side lights (second position).

The circuit is protected by two specific fuses housed in the body computer node, used individually for each individual light cluster.

The 'Follow me home' function is designed to keep the dipped headlamps and the side lights on after the engine has been switched off for a time of 30 seconds or multiples thereof.

  • ignition in OFF position
  • when the light flasher is operated within 2 minutes of turning the key to STEP (increase of 30 seconds per operation to a maximum of 210 seconds)

Function programming is canceled by maintaining the light flasher operated for longer than 2 seconds.

The instrument panel dsiplays the function activation time.

For further details, see the relevant function under DESCRIPTION AND OPERATION.
