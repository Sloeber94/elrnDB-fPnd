Refitting

Before carrying out the operations described below, make sure that the window is fully closed.

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

5570T93 REAR SIDE LOUDSPEAKER. (one), left or right - R.R. with rear door panel or wing. removed

7010A05 rear side door water guard protection-r r or arrange

7010E93 partition on rear door, lh or rh - r r with door panel removed

1. Working from inside the door frame, release the retaining clip (1a) and disconnect the opening link from handle to lock (1b), handle side.

2. Undo the retaining bolts.

3. Remove the outer handle on the rear door.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the outer handle of the rear door in its seating and tighten the fixing bolts.

Working from inside the door frame, connect the opening link from handle to lock and engage the retaining clip.

7010E93 partition on rear door, lh or rh - r r with door panel removed

7010A05 rear side door water guard protection-r r or arrange

5570T93 REAR SIDE LOUDSPEAKER. (one), left or right - R.R. with rear door panel or wing. removed

Connect the battery negative terminal.
