Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Raise the vehicle.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and nuts (1b).

2. Release and remove the rear suspension anti-roll bar.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: 4x4
