The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1108 - Warning light serial line"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error is indicated if the control unit cannot communicate with the instrument panel

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

CHECK PANEL INITIAL CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Turn the key ON and check that the instrument panel carries out the initial check on the warning lights

Move on to Step 3 1

Failure to carry out the initial check

Move on to Step 2 1

3

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the electrical continuity of the serial line between the ABS control unit and the body computer

See E7023 ESP

Move on to Step 4 1

Electrical continuity failure between the ABS control unit and the body computer

Restore the correct connection and move on to Step 4

4

Problems encountered

Operation

Clear the errors using the Examiner

Turn the key OFF and then ON to ensure that the control unit checks whether the errors are still present.

Check that the fault is no longer present
