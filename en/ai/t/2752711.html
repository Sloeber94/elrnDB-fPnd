3310A38 front brake caliper, removed (one) l/h or r/h - overhaul

Path

Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Using the special spanner, undo the connector on the caliper and remove the pipe.

2. Place the front brake caliper in the vice and remove the bleed valve (2a) complete with cover (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the piston from the front brake caliper casing using a jet of compressed air in the brake fluid inlet port.

Remove the piston carefully to prevent the spraying of brake fluid, damage or scratches.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the piston protective boot.

2. Remove the gasket.

During the extraction of the gasket, take care not to scratch the brake caliper body cylinder.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Fit the gasket in the housing in the front brake caliper casing.

Fit the protective boot in the piston splining.

Fit the piston - protective boot assembly in the front brake caliper casing.

Fit the bleed valve complete with cover.

Using a special spanner, tighten the brake pipe connector to torque.
