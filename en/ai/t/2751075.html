K10 DOORS OPEN CHECK NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the Body Computer does not display that one or more of the doors or the boot may not be properly shut. The latter are open, but the Body Computer does not light up the doors ajar warning light

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

Operation
