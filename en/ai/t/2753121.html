7015A24 tailgate hinges - repair or replace with tailgate removed

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If the vehicle is equipped with an airbag system, before carrying out any work the following preliminary operations must be carried out; (1) turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it; (2) disconnect the battery terminals and insulate them; (3) wait at least 10 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES (see assembly 5508C - AIR BAG SYSTEM)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
