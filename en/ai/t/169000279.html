Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst driving, especially on unmade roads, there are transverse vibrations at the front section. This diagnosis can only be made after

See table S15 RUMOROSITA' IN CONDIZIONI DI MARCIA

Carry out the latter first.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Having carried out the road test, during the

See table S15 RUMOROSITA' IN CONDIZIONI DI MARCIA

transverse vibrations are felt from the front end.

Continue depending on the circumstances in which the vibration occurs

End of diagnosis

  • The vibration is noticeable during take off
  • The vibration is accompanied by noise which increases with speed
  • The vibration is accompanied by noise over unmade roads
  • Move on to Step 4
  • Move on to Step 2
  • Move on to Step 1

1

WHEEL SIDE CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Raise the vehicle on a two column lift and check:

  • That the driveshaft-wheel side joint matching is correct
  • That the seal is present

Move on to Step 2

  • Incorrect matching
  • Sealing ring missing
  • Replace the constant velocity joint and the driveshaftOp. 2710A10 DRIVE SHAFT WITH JOINTS (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R
  • Replace the constant velocity joint and the driveshaftOp. 2710A10 DRIVE SHAFT WITH JOINTS (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R

2

WHEEL SIDE JOINT CLEARANCE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the clearance for the wheel side constant velocity joint is not too great

Move on to Step 3

Excessive clearance

Replace the constant velocity joint and grease correctly

Op. 2710A20 WHEEL END CONSTANT VELOCITY JOINT (ONE), L/H OR R/H - R + R WITH DRIVE SHAFT REMOVED - INCLUDES BOOT REPLACEMENT

4

WHEEL SIDE JOINT TIGHTENING CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the wheel

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R

Check that the constant velocity joint fastening nut is tightened and that the staking is in place.

Move on to Step 5

Nut loosening

Replace the nut, restore the torque tightening and stake

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

The fault is due to another system (engine, clutch, gearbox, differential, suspension).

Search for the cause in one of these systems, following the appropriate diagnostic procedures
