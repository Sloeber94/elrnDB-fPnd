7005A10 front side door - adjust striker and adapt seal in necessary

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before altering the position of the door striker, ensure the door is properly aligned, as described in the procedure:

7005A14 front side door - adjust centering and moulding - includes adaptation of hinges and seals on door compartment

1. Loosen the bolts.

2. Use the bolt slots to move the striker into the correct position.

Fit shims if necessary in order to adjust the striker accurately. Permitted adjustment movements are indicated by arrows.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Check the striker and catch fit together properly and are aligned when the door is closed.

Tighten the striker bolts to the specified torque.
