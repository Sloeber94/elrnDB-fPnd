Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Parking lights do not work under any circumstances.

The parking light activation signal (side and number plate lights) is sent directly from the Body Computer Node.

The parking lights are switched on by the driver using the steering column switch unit. The side lights are activated with the key in the OFF position:

  • with the steering column switch unit in a neutral position, all the side lights come on
  • with the steering column switch unit turned to the left or the right, the left or right side lights, respectively, come on

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • Ring nut on the side lights and stalk unit turned to the right: right lights
