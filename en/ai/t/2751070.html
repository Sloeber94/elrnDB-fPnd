Applies to: all engines; all production periods

During driving the rev counter does not work, or it works irregularly.

The speed message is supplied by the Brake Node and passes through the Body Computer Node being made available to the Instrument Panel Node via the network. It should be remembered that in the case of a C-CAN error, the signal is supplied by the Brake Node to the Body Computer via line F and made available on the B-CAN from the Body Computer Node.
