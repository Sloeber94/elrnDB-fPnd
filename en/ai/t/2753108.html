7010A05 rear side door water guard protection-r r or arrange

Position the glass in such a way as to be able to gain access to the safety retainer.

1. Release the safety retainer (1a) and release the window (1b) from the window opening device.

Raise the glass fully and support it using suitable suction pads.

2. Unscrew the retaining nuts.

3. Extract the mechanical window opening mechanism from the inside of the door frame.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the mechanical window opening mechanism inside the door frame and tighten the fixing nuts.

Support the window manually and remove the suction pads fitted previously.

Lower the window sliding it slowly as far as the safety retainer.

Connect the drive pin to the window opening device and attach the safety retainer.

Check that the window opening device is working properly and that the glass is sliding correctly.
