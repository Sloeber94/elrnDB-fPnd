The diagnostic equipment signals the following errors: "C1101 - Engine CAN Messages Error" and "U1701 - Engine management node missing error"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

error C1101 is signalled if the brake control unit detects invalid messages from the Engine Management control unit.Error U1701 is signalled if the control unit does not detect the Engine Management control unit.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
