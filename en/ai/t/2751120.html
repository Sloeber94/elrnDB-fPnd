Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the radio front panel buttons are pressed/adjusted, the corresponding functions take place. The corresponding function does not take place when the buttons on the steering wheel are operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

When the steering wheel buttons are activated, check that the corresponding operation is correct.

Continue to Step 2

One of the steering wheel controls faulty.

Continue to Step 2

All of the steering wheel controls faulty.

Continue to Step 3

2

STEERING WHEEL MODULE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • Steering wheel module correct power supply
  • Continuity of the wiring.
  • Operation of the buttons on the steering wheel See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 4

Wiring faulty.

Restore the correct operation of the wiring

Wiring faulty.

Repair/replace the controls on the steering wheel

3

RADIO MODULE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check at the radio connector that the signals are correctly received from the module on the steering wheel See E3510 RADIO

Continue to Step 4

Radio controls from steering wheel conversion module faulty.

Replace the radio Op. 5570T80 car radio unit - r+r
