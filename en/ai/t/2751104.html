The Headlamp Alignment Failure message is displayed through a direct command between the Headlamp Alignment Corrector and the Instrument Panel Node

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check that there are no faults in the errors ambient at the headlamp alignment correction control unit

Continue to Step 3

Presence of faults

Continue as instructed by the tester

Warning light faulty

Continue to Step 2

2

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the electrical connection (continuity/insulation to earth) between the Headlamp Alignment Correction Control Unit and the Instrument Panel Node See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Continue to Step 3

Cable directly earthed/broken

Renew the electrical connections See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

3

Continue as instructed by the tester

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the instrument panel Op. 5560B11
