BRAKE LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • connections between brake pedal and Body Computer;
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs
  • bulb earth See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 3

Defective component

Replace the component involved (if a fuse is involved, search for the cause of the problem)

Connections broken

Restore the connections See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

3

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual defective bulbs and the bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 4

Side bulb damaged

Change the bulb Op. 5540A50 TAIL-LIGHT BULB (ONE), LH OR RH - REPLACE

Side bulb damaged

Change the bulb Op. 5550D17 third brake light - r r

Side bulb damaged

Replace the bulb holder assembly Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

Side bulb damaged

Replace the third brake light Op. 5550D17 third brake light - r r

4
