1

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Activate the self-diagnostic procedure and check that the speakers are operating correctly when on SETUP screen 5.

See Test 5580PJ CONNECT SELF-DIAGNOSIS PROCEDURE

Withdraw the Connect unit from the facia and check 8-way connection B is correctly inserted

See E3510 RADIO

Check the continuity of the wiring to the speakers.

Check the audio controls on the steering wheel if present

See Test 5570TD CHECKING THE OPERATION OF THE STEERING-WHEEL CONTROL FROM THE CAR RADIO

Move on to Step 2

  • Speakers defective
  • Connection on Connect incorrectly connected
  • Speaker wiring interrupted
  • Audio controls on steering wheel defective
  • Replace the speakers
  • Connect the connection correctly
  • Restore wiring continuitySee E3510 RADIO
  • Restore operation
