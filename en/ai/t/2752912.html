Removing

Refitting

Using a suitable tool, prise off the front window washer.

1. Disconnect the tube.

2. Remove the front windscreen washer jet.

Do the same to remove the remaining front windscreen washer jet.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Connect the tube to the front windscreen washer jet.

Fit the front windscreen washer jet in its seating and check that it is secure.

Do the same to refit the remaining front windscreen washer jet.
