The diagnostic equipment signals one or more of the following errors: "C1070 ABS valve, right front discharging", "C1075, ABS valve, right front pressurizing", "C1060 ABS valve, left front discharging", "C1065 ABS valve, left front pressurizing", "C1090 ABS valve, right rear discharging", "C1095 ABS valve, right rear pressurizing", "C1080 ABS valve, left rear discharging" and/or "C1085 ABS valve, left rear pressurizing"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error is signalled if the ABS solenoid valves cannot be activated. This means that there is an open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the solenoid valves or the connection
