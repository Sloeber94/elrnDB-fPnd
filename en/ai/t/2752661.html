Removing

Position the gearbox and differential assembly fastened to the tools used for the removal in the engine compartment, and fit it in place taking care not to damage surrounding components.

Centre the gearbox-differential unit on the centring pins and secure it, tightening the lower nut and bolts to torque.

To facilitate centring the pins on the engine and the gearbox shaft and the clutch driven plate, move the gearbox-differential unit slightly.
