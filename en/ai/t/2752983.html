Before working on the Air Bag system, it is essential to carry out the following preliminary operations: (1) turn the ignition off and remove the key; (2) disconnect the battery terminals and insulate them; (3) wait at least 10 minutes before proceeding.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES (see assembly 5508C - AIR BAG SYSTEM).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Slacken the bolt on the sealing collar.

Rotate the steering column switch unit slightly in both directions to release it from the centring lug on the steering column.

2. Remove the steering column switch complete with clock spring.

The clock spring is fitted with a device which automatically stops its rotation when it is removed. Avoid forcing its rotation.

If the stalk unit is reused, it is advisable to secure the two plates in an appropriate manner, to avoid them rotating accidentally.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
