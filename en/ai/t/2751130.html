Applies to: all engines; all production periods

There is water penetration through the sun roof;

the roof in question is not equipped with drainage pipes therefore any water penetration is due to faults in the seals and/or incorrect matching

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

SEAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the perimeter water seal making sure that it is fitted correctly

Continue to Step 2

Seal faulty

Replace the seal fitting it correctly

Drip channel incorrectly fitted

Refit the drip channel properly (Op. 7055A14)

2

REAR GLASS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the sealant matching between the fixed rear glass and the roof frame

Continue to Step 3

Matching/sealant not correct

Restore correct coupling

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Continue to Step 4

4

ROOF FITTING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the roof is correctly fitted in the special housing and that there are no visible areas where the sealant is not correct

Continue to Step 3

Incorrect fitting

Refit correctly
