Release the front and rear door housing seals as necessary in order to be able to release the pillar trim lower element.

1. Undo the rear panel fixing bolt.

Move the seat belt lower branch aside.

2. Release the fixing buttons using the special tool.

Removing

Move the seat belt lower branch aside.

1. Place the centre pillar trim lower element (1a) in position fitting the pins (1b) in the special openings (1c) and securing them using the special fixing buttons.

2. Tighten the rear panel fixing bolt.
