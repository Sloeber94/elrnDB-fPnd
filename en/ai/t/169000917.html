3350EB STEERING ANGLE SENSOR OPERATION CHECK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Road test the vehicle driving along a straight section at a speed of approximately 50 km/h.

- Make sure that it will not cause a danger to traffic and then steer to the left and the right several times. It is NOT necessary to steer in an exaggerated manner to cause to the ESP warning light to come on.
