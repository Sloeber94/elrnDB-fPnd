Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Road test the vehicle driving along a straight section of road with an even surface at a speed of approximately 50 km/h.

Make sure first that it is safe, as regards the traffic situation, and then steer several times to the left and the right trying to reproduce the situation that has produced the error.
