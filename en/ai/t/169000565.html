The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1131 - Pressure Sensor"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

This error may be signalled if there is an open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the wiring or the brake lights switch, if the pressure sensor, the reading circuit or the brake lights switch is damaged, if the sensor does not receive the correct power supply or if the brake lights switch is not correctly calibrated

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

ELECTRICAL CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the electrical connections for the brake lights circuit are correct

Check the continuity of the wiring between the pressure sensor and control unit

See E7023 ESP

Also check that the brake lights are not burnt out

Move on to Step 2 1

  • Wiring faulty
  • Brake lights burnt out
  • Repair the wiring
  • Replace the burnt out bulb and move on to Step 5
