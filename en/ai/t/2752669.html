Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7040A28 TRIM ON GEARBOX CONTROL PANEL - R.R.

1. Disconnect the ends of the gear engagement and selection flexible rods.

2. Remove the retaining clips for the gear engagement and selection rods.

Release the gear engagement and selection flexible rods.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

Op. 1048A10 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE

Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

Op. 5530B52 BATTERY CRADLE - R R

1. Disconnect the ends of the gear engagement/selector cables.

2. Remove the clips (2a) and release the gear engagement/selection cables (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

Raise the vehicle.

1. Release the gear engagement and selection flexible rods from the fastening clip on the suspension frame.

Release the rubber buffer in the housing in the vehicle floor.

2. Release the gear engagement and selection flexible rods and remove them.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the gear engagement and selection flexible rods.

Fit the rubber buffer in the housing in the vehicle floor.

Secure the gear engagement and selection flexible rods to the fastening clip on the suspension frame.

Lower the vehicle.

Connect the ends of the gear engagement and selection flexible rods and secure the retaining clips.

Op. 5530B52 BATTERY CRADLE - R R

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

Op. 5530B10 BATTERY - R+R

Op. 1048A10 AIR CLEANER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE

Connect the ends of the gear engagement and selection rods.

Secure the gear engagement and selection rods using the special fastening clips.

Op. 7040A28 TRIM ON GEARBOX CONTROL PANEL - R.R.

Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods
