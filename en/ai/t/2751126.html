The sun roof is noisy during opening/closing and/or whilst driving

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the noise only occurs during the opening/closing of the roof and is not there whilst driving with the roof closed.

Continue to Step 2

The noise is also present whilst driving with the roof closed.

Continue to Step 1

1

PROFILE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the profile between the front moving glass and the rear fixed glass is correct

Continue to Step 2

Profile not correct

Restore correct coupling

2

SLIDES CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the slides; in particular, make sure that:

  • there are no obstacles or impediments to the movement of the slides
  • the splining on the slides are properly greased
  • the matching between the splining and the pins for the hinged opening is correct

Continue to Step 3

Presence of impediments/obstacles

Restore correct operation

Splining not greased

Grease the splining

Faulty match between slide components

Restore correct operation, if necessary, replace the damaged slide

3

MOTOR TIGHTENING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the bolts fixing the motor are correctly tightened using a torque wrench

Continue to Step 4

Tightening not correct/incomplete

Tighten the bolts to the correct torque

4

MOVEMENT CONTROL CONDITION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the control racks and the motor ring gear

Continue to Step 5

Rack faulty

Restore correct operation, if necessary replace the rack Op. 7030B45

Motor ring gear faulty

Restore correct operation, if necessary replace the motor Op. 7030B70 sun-roof control switch - r+r

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the sun roof electric motor Op. 7030B70 sun-roof control switch - r+r
