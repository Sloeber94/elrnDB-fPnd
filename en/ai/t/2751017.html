CONTROL UNIT ERRORS CHECK

Operation

Check that there are no control unit errors. In particular:

  • Clutch sensor error
  • Clutch solenoid current error

Vehicle speed error

in the presence of Clutch sensor and/or clutch solenoid valve current errors, for safety reasons (at speeds below 5 km/h) the Selespeed control unit could request the injection control unit to switch off the engine; it is therefore advisable to carry out an engine management fault diagnosis.
