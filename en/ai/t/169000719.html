Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1133 - Clutch switch"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

This error is signalled if the clutch switch is not working properly. The clutch switch is used for the operation of the Hill Holder.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CLUTCH PEDAL SWITCH OPERATION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the Examiner, enter into the fault diagnosis in the "PARAMETERS" ambient (Engine management) and check the "CLUTCH PEDAL STATUS" parameter.

Press the clutch pedal and read "PRESSED".

Release the clutch pedal and read "RELEASED"

Move on to Step 4 1

Incorrect reading

Replace the sensor.

Move on to Step 2 1

2

CLUTCH SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Detach the connector for the switch and check its operation

Move on to Step 2 1

The switch is not working properly

Replace the switch

Move on to Step 4 1

3

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the electrical continuity between the clutch switch and the engine management control unit.

Move on to Step 4 1

Electrical continuity failure between the switch and the engine management control unit

Restore the connection.

Move on to Step 4 1

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Road test the vehicle to check that the system is working properly

End of diagnosis
