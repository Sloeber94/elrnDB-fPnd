Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean the area of the wheelarch where the moulding will be bonded and remove any previous adhesive residues (where fastened to the same wheelarch).

1. Position the moulding (1a) in its seat and secure using adhesive (1b) and studs (1c).

If the moulding removed previously is re-used, clean off any adhesive traces and apply a new adhesive with the same properties.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV
