Removing

Refitting

1. Release the fastening clip (1a) and release the cold air intake hose (1b) from the cold air intake.

2. Release the oil vapour recovery hose from the air filter casing.

3. Undo the fixing bolts (3a) and remove the air filter (3b) complete with cold air intake hose.

Separate the air filter assembly from the cold air intake hose at the bench.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Refitting

2. Release the oil vapour recovery hose from the air filter casing.

3. Undo the fixing bolts (3a) and remove the air filter (3b) complete with cold air intake hose.

Separate the air filter assembly from the cold air intake hose at the bench.
