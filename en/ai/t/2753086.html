7010B14 rear side door catch striker r r

Undo the bolt securing the door check strap to the bodyshell, and close the door to bring in the door check strap.

Loosen the bolts securing the moving hinges to the door.

Adjust the door position, making use of the bolt slots, so that it is perfectly aligned with the body.

It is advisable to work on one hinge at a time.

Tighten the bolts securing the moving hinges to the door at the specified torque.
