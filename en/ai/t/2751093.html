Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The warning light stays on even when the handbrake is off.

A parking brake warning light activation command is sent by the switch on the lever to the Body Computer Node, which makes it available to the Instrument Panel Node via the B-CAN.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the earth insulation of the wiring between Parking Brake lever switch and Body Computer.

Continue to Step 2

Wiring not insulated

Renew the wiring See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

2

SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the switch on the parking brake lever

Continue to Step 3

Faulty switch

Replace switch Op. 5550D18

3

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check, the operation of the parking brake warning light, in active diagnosis, at the Instrument Panel Node

Continue to Step 4

The warning light does not flash

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keep the tester connected and check parameter plausibility on the Body Computer

Continue to Step 5

Parameter not plausible

Replace the Body Computer Op. 5505A32 body computer - r r

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
