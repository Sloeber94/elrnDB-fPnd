Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1315 Slewing sensor"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error is signalled if the sensor voltage is < 0.3 V or > 4.7 V or if the value sent by the sensor is not plausible. This means that there is an open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the sensor or the wiring, that the sensor is mechanically damaged or that the reading circuit is damaged

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

SENSOR COMPATIBILITY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the design number for the slewing sensor is compatible with the part number for the Model-Version-Series in question

Move on to Step 2

The design number is not compatible

Replace the slewing sensor with one that is compatible for the Model-Version-Series

Op. 3350E30 LATERAL ACCELERATION AND YAW SENSOR R R

Move on to Step 5
