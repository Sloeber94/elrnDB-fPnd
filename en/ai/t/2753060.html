5570T91 Front SIDE LOUDSPEAKER. (one), left or right - R.R. with door panel removed

7005A04 front side door water guard protection - r r or replace with door panel removed

Posistion the window to gain access to the safety retainer.

1. Disengate the safety retainer (1a) and disengage the window glass (1b) from the window lift system (1c).

Fully lift the window glass and support it with suitable suction pads.

2. Unscrew the retaining nuts.

3. Remove the mechanical window lift mechanism from the door frame.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the mechanical window lift mechanism in the door frame and tighten the fixing nuts.

Manually support the window glass and remove the suction pads mounted previously.

Lower the window glass, sliding it slowly to the safety retainer.

Connect the window lift drive pin and engage the safety retainer.

Check that the window lift system is working correctly, and that the window glass is sliding properly.
