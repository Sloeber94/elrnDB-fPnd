Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the display does not display the time correctly or at all.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

INSTRUMENT PANEL FUNCTIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • That the display shows the correct time
  • That it is possible to adjust the time
  • That the clock moves forward
  • That the instrument panel light dimmer has not been selected.
  • That the mode button has not been pressed.
  • That there are no failure messages displayed.

Continue to Step 2

The time displayed is not correct

Adjust the time

The time cannot be adjusted

Continue to Step 2

The clock is stuck

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11

The time is not displayed because there are priority messages or adjustments in progress.

Remove the cause of the priority messages

The display/time cannot be seen properly

Continue to Step 2

Problems met

Continue to Step 3

There are errors

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment
