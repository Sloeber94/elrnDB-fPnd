The Cruise Control system controls do not work under any circumstances.

Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the engine management control unit

Continue to Step 2

An error is detected in the control unit

Proceed according to the instructions of the diagnostic equipment

Lack of dialogue with the control unit

Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

2

CHECK CRUISE CONTROL SELECTOR

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the Cruise Control contacts for the ON/OFF control are working See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Continue to Step 3

Lack of continuity

Replace the stalk unit lever with built-in Cruise Control system control Op. 5580A10

3

CHECK ON SUPPLY

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the ignition and Cruise Control on, check that there is a voltage signal from the Cruise Control system control to the engine management control unit:

Keeping the ignition key in the ON position, check that there is a voltage signal from the brake pedal switch to the engine management control unit See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Continue to Step 6

No voltage from the Cruise Control system control

Continue to Step 4

No voltage from the brake pedal switch

Continue to Step 5

4

CHECK ON CONNECTIONS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the electrical continuity of the wiring between the ignition switch, junction unit, Cruise Control system control and engine management control unit. See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Continue to Step 6

Lack of continuity

Restore the connections See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

5

CHECK ON CONNECTIONS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the brake pedal switch.

Check the electrical continuity of the wiring between the ignition switch, junction unit, brake pedal switch and engine management control unit See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Continue to Step 6

Brake switch faulty

Replace the brake pedal switch Op. 5550D10 brake light switch - r+r

Lack of continuity

Restore the connections See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL
