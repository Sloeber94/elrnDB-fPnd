Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Air Conditioner and Smart gearbox and ESP

Description

The engine cooling system consists of a fan operated by the engine control unit, in accordance with the parameters supplied by the coolant temperature sensor and the linear pressure sensor.

The operation of the fan is controlled by two specific relays, located in the engine compartment junction unit.

The fan supply line and the relay energisation circuit are protected by two specific fuses located in the engine compartment junction unit.

The fan has two operating speeds, activated by the engine control unit in accordance with data received by the water temperature sensor and linear pressure sensor.
