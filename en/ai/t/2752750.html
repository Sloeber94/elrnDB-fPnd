1. Release the pipe from the clips.

2. Remove clip (2a), unscrew connector (2b) joining it to the flexible section, then remove the right rigid front brake pipe (2c) from the engine compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Working from the engine compartment, fit the right rigid front brake pipe.

Working from the right front wheel arch, tighten the rigid pipe to the flexible section of the left front brake pipe to torque.
