Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The car is fitted with numerous electronic control units that perform a self-diagnostic function.

When a diagnostic tester (Examiner or other device) is connected to these control units, self-diagnosis data can be read (parameters, errors) or the available active diagnostic tests can be carried out.

Each control unit converts its self-diagnostic line into a single connector that can be connected to the diagnostic tester.

The electrical connector for the diagnostic socket is incorporated in the junction unit under the dashboard located on the lower part of the left front pillar.

The following control units are connected to the diagnostic connector:

  • Air Bag control unit;
  • electric steering control unit;
  • engine management control unit;
  • body computer Node;
  • parking control unit.
