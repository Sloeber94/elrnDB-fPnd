1020B12 CHAIN-DRIVE VALVE GEAR COVER - R.R. AND GASKET REPLACEMENT FOR MODELS WITH AIR-CONDITIONER

1. Undo the bolt securing the driven timing pulley.

Mark the installation position of the exhaust side toothed drive pulley, the toothed drive pulley and the timing drive chain (if the removed chain is being reused); If the pulleys are replaced, fit them with the writing turned to the outside.

2. Remove the timing chain (2a) together with the driven timing pulley (2b) and the toothed drive pulley (2c).

Using suitable equipment, prepare the moving tensioner and keep it fully retracted using a suitable retaining pin.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the timing chain (1a) together with the driven timing pulley (1b) and the toothed drive pulley (1c).

2. Tighten the bolt securing the toothed driven pulley to the specified torque.
