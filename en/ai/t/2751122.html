Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The sun roof does not move at all when the opening/closing switches are pressed.

The sun roof is controlled by a control unit that receives a direct power supply protected by a fuse and an ignition-controlled supply (+15) in order to allow the operation of the roof with the ignition on.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
