7005B14 front side door catch striker r r

Undo the bolt securing the door check strap to the bodyshell, and shut the door to make the check strap go in.

Undo the bolts securing the moving hinges to the door.

Adjust the door position, making use of the bolt slots, so that it is perfectly aligned with the body.

It is advisable to work on one hinge at a time.

Tighten the bolts securing the moving hinges to the door to the specified torque.
