Unscrew the aerial and remove.

1. Use a blade to cut the adhesive and remove the moulding/oddment holder.

Refitting

Removing

Carefully clean the area of the roof where the moulding/oddment holder will be bonded and remove any previous adhesive residues (if bonded to the same door).

1. Position the moulding/oddment holder in its seat and secure with adhesive.

If the moulding/oddment holder removed previously is re-used, clean off any adhesive traces and apply a new adhesive with the same properties.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV

1 Tighten the fixing bolts.

2. Fit the rear carrier bar panels in their seats.
