Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two extra-penetration fog lights in the front light clusters for use in the case of poor visibility. The fog lamps are activated by a button on the control panel.

The fog lamp circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a relay, both located in the underfacia junction unit.

The side lights must be switched on in order to switch the fog lights on.

If the ignition key is switched OFF or the side lights are turned off, the fog lights are deactivated even if the operating button is still pressed:

Fog lamp activation is indicated by a warning light in the instrument panel.
