X01 EXCESSIVE FUEL CONSUMPTION

Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

The engine appears to running smoothly, but the fuel consumption is higher than previously recorded during the life of the vehicle.

The consumption data in the vehicle technical documentation has been defined during the course of homologation tests in accordance with directive 93/116/CE. These tests simulate an urban cycle and an 'average' extra-urban cycle and therefore may not be indicative as consumption depends very much on the style of driving.

Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

HIGH PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket.:

  • Check as described in the test See Test 1060D Check high pressure fuel circuit that the diesel pressure parameter is correct.
  • Check the operation of the rail high pressure sensor by carrying out the test. See Test 1060GE Check on operation of diesel high pressure sensor
  • Check the operation of the pressure regulator through active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 5

Sensor faulty

Replace sensor.

Pressure regulator faulty

Replace the pressure regulator
