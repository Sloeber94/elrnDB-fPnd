Refitting

Removing

1. Before fitting the toothed timing belt, check that the references are lined up, making sure that the mark on the driven pulley (1a) lines up with the mark on the cylinder head (1b) and the mark on the drive pulley (1c) lines up with the reference (1d) on the oil pump casing.

If the reference marks do not line up, move both the crankshaft and the camshaft very small amounts until the marks line up.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods
