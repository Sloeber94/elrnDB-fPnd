Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The sun roof is the 'Skydome' type, comprising two glass panels, one fixed and one moving.

The sun roof control unit is incorporated in the electric motor that controls the opening/closing of the actual roof; the operation of the roof takes place via the switch, connected directly to the control unit.

If the sun roof gets stuck whilst closing, due to the presence of an obstacle, the sun roof control unit (incorporated in the electric motor) interrupts the movement, keeping it in the position reached.

If the sun roof gets stuck whilst opening, the control unit interrupts the movement of the glass panel and reverses the direction, moving it 10 mm forward from the reversal point.
