7050A19 rear seat squab - r r

Raise the head restraint to the end of travel position.

1. Press at the points shown by the arrows to release the head restraint pins from the guide and retaining bushes.

2. Remove the rear seat head restraints.

2. Remove the (left and right) protection for the rear seat backrest attachment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Take great care not to damage the backrest cover.
