7040B10 load platform (luggage platform) trim - r+r

1. Undo the nuts fixing the support platform.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the support platform.

3. Remove the support platform from the rear of the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: VAN Configuration

If necessary, remove the bracket underneath as described below:

1. Undo the bracket fixing bolts.

2. Remove the bolts using a drill fitted with a suitable bit.

3. Remove the mounting bracket from the load carrying platform.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: VAN Configuration

Refitting

Removing

Fit the bracket and tighten the fixing bolts.

Tighten the new bolt until the head of the outer bolt breaks.

Place the support platform in the load carrying compartment.

Tighten the retaining bolts and nuts.
