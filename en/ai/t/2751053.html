With the ignition key in the ON position, when reverse gear is engaged, the reversing light does not come on

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner Plus or Smart) to the Body Computer and check that there are no faults

Continue to Step 1

the operation of the lights in active diagnosis.

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

1

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the reversing bulb and the right rear light cluster

Continue to Step 2

Bulb damaged

Change the bulb Op. 5550D26

Bulb damaged

Renew or replace the light cluster Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

2

REVERSING LIGHT SWITCH CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the reversing light switch is correctly fastened and working properly

Continue to Step 3

Reversing light switch faulty

Renew or replace the reversing light switch Op. 5550D22

3

CHECK ON REVERSING LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check, in order:

  • reversing light fuse (services)
  • connections between Body Computer and reversing light switch;
  • connections between Body Computer and reversing light bulb;
  • bulb earth See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Connections broken

Replace the fuse (and search for the cause of its blowing)

Connections broken

Restore the connections See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Connections broken

Repair the earth contact See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

4

Continue as instructed by the tester

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
