Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The brake lights are located at the rear in the side lights and in the central third brake light.

All three bulbs are operated whenever the brake pedal is pressed, by means of a switch on the pedal itself.

The circuit is supplied with the ignition switched ON and is protected by a fuse, in the junction unit under the facia.

The braking signal is also taken from the ABS system (where fitted).
