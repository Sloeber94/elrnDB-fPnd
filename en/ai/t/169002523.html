Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

There are three radio versions:

  • radio with cassette player (radio tape);
  • radio with CD player (radio CD);
  • radio with CD-MP3 player (radio CD-MP3).

The radio CD and radio CD-MP3 versions are equipped for connection via the CAN to allow dialogue with the other system nodes and obtain the follow information:

  • anti-theft code;
  • on control (for switching off logic);
  • speed dependent volume control
  • lighting control.

The general specifications of the radio are:

  • 4x40W audio power;
  • 7 band graphic equalizer;
  • integrated alphanumerical display;
  • remote control from steering wheel;
  • possibility of interface with external CD-Changer via ASCI-BUS line.

There are two types of acoustic system available on the vehicle:

  • standard;
  • Hi-Fi (Sound System).

The standard system comprises four speakers, two located in the front doors and two in the rear doors or side panels.

The Hi-Fi system comprises six speakers and a sub-woofer box (housing a power amplifier).
