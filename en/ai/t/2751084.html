Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

With the ignition ON the warning light in the panel is on during the check stage.

The warning light on signal is made available by the Engine Management Node to the C-CAN; the Body Computer Node makes the message available to the B-CAN and consequently to the instrument panel;

Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket and check at the instrument panel node that the warning light is working properly through active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 2

Warning light not working

Replace instrument panel Op. 5560B11

2

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Keeping the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) connected to the diagnostic socket, check at the engine management node that the indicator and the temperature warning light are working properly through active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 3

Indicator not working

Replace the engine control unit Op. 1056B82 injection/ignition system e.c.u. (one) - r + r

Continue as instructed by the tester
