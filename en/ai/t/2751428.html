Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The left and right direction indicators are operated by raising or lowering the appropriate lever on the stalk unit.

The hazard warning lights (right and left direction indicators operated simultaneously) are turned on by the pressing the red button at the centre of the dashboard.

The turn signals are activated with the ignition key on; For safety reasons, the hazard warning lights are supplied directly from the battery.

Two warning lights on the dashboard, also flashing, indicate when the left and right direction indicators are turned on - they come on simultaneously if the hazard warning lights are switched on.

The body computer node

  • turns on individual turn signals located in the front light clusters, in th e tail-lights and in the side turn signals;
  • monitors correct operation of the system and indicates any faults by turning on the lighting failure warning ilght on the instrument panel.
