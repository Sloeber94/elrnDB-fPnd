Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The horns are operated by pressing the switch on the steering wheel.

The horn circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a specific relay located in the engine compartment junction unit.

For safety reasons, the horns can be operated at any time (even with the key removed).
