Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle has two additional fog lamps for use in poor visibility conditions. The fog lights are activated by the button on the control panel.

The fog lights circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a relay, both located in the junction unit in the engine compartment.

The fog lights only come on when the side lights are switched on.

In addition, if the key is turned ot the OFF position or the side lights are switched off, the fog lamps are switched off even if the button is pressed.

Foglamp activation is indicated by activation of a warning light on the control panel.
