PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Having carried out the road test, during the

Vedere tabella S15 RUMOROSITA' IN CONDIZIONI DI MARCIA

, there was a whistling noise from the front.

Continue depending on the circumstances in which the noise occurs.

End of diagnosis

  • The noise increases when cornering
  • The noise increases in vertical oscillation conditions
  • Replace the wheel bearing on the side where the noise is loudest.
  • Move on to Step 1

2

SHOCK ABSORBER STEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the wheel

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R

Check that the noise is not due to irregular friction between the shock absorber stem and the gasket, checking the condition of the stem chrome surface.

End of diagnosis

There is irregular friction

Lubricate the stem raising the dust cover with a layer of silicon grease.

If the noise persists, replace the shock absorber
