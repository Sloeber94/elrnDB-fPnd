Removing

Refitting

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

5570T91 Front SIDE LOUDSPEAKER. (one), left or right - R.R. with door panel removed

Removing the water shield is a destructive operation. To avoid harmful leaks, a new shield must be fitted.

1. Using a suitable tool, cut the bead of sealant and remove the water shield protection.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean the door frame from adhesive residues.

Check that the new seal is intact and not torn.

Position the loudspeaker wiring and link from internal handle to door catch in their seatings in the water shield protection.

Fit the new water shield, making sure that the adhesive bead adheres to the door frame.

5570T91 Front SIDE LOUDSPEAKER. (one), left or right - R.R. with door panel removed

Connect the battery negative terminal.
