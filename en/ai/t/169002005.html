The control button activates the passenger compartment fan properly but the temperature of the air is not correct.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

Results of the tests

1

SYSTEM PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the climate control system to the special repressurizing equipment and check the pressures

See Test 5040OB AIR- CONDITIONER SYSTEM OPERATION CHECK (PRESSURES)

Move on to Step 2

System pressure not correct.

Restore the correct system pressure value

Refrigerant pressurizing too low or too high

Restore the correct coolant level

Op. 0010T84 COOLANT FOR AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM DEHYDRATE AND RECHARGE

2

CHECK ON CORRECT MANUAL CONTROL OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Before starting, check the following conditions:

  • ambient temperature around 20 °C
  • vehicle with doors and windows closed (during the cooling and measuring of the temperature)

With the engine on, at ambient temperature (check using the diagnostic equipment that the passenger compartment temperature and evaporator sensors indicate about 20 °C), with the vehicle compressor on, adjust the temperature to maximum cold LO, the distribution to the front position, the speed for the selector to the 2nd speed and the outside air intake function. After about 5 minutes in these conditions, check using the diagnostic equipment that the temperature values for the passenger compartment and evaporator sensors show a decrease: from the initial temperature of about 20 °C (ambient temp.) and gradually decrease to a temperature of about 3.5°C for the evaporator sensor and between 6 and 10°C for the front distribution air vents. Check whether the temperature of the air coming out of the vents actually alters (becoming colder)

Move on to Step 5

The air coming out of the climate control vents does not feel colder
