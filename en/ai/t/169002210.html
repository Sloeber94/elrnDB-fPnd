Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Removing

Refitting

1. Operate the brake pedal to release clip (1a), remove pin (1b) and release fork (1c) from the connection shaft.

2. Unscrew the pedal retaining bolt and remove.

3. Remove the brake pedal.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Refitting

Removing

Position the brake pedal in the vehicle.

Fit the pedal retaining pin fully home to the end of its travel.

Position the connection shaft fork, insert the retaining pin and then do up the spring.
