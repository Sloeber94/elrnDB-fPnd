I01 SIDE LIGHTS/NO. PLATE LIGHTS NOT WORKING

Path

fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)

Interactive page

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the steering column switch unit ring nut is operated, the side lights or the no. plate lights do not work.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the Examiner to the diagnostic socket

Check:

  • that there are no faults in the Body Computer
  • the operation of the lights in active diagnosis.

Continue to Step 3

Body Computer faulty

Continue according to the diagnostic equipment instructions

2

CHECK ON INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the individual bulbs and complete bulb holder assembly

Continue to Step 3

No. plate lights faulty

Replace the bulb Op. 5540B32

No. plate lights faulty

Replace the bulb Op. 5540A52

No. plate lights faulty

Repair or replace front lights cluster Op. 5540B08

No. plate lights faulty

Repair or replace front lights cluster Op. 5540B07

No. plate lights faulty

Repair or replace the rear light cluster Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

No. plate lights faulty

Renew or replace the no. plate lights Op. 5540A94 l/h or r/h number plate light (one) - r+r

3

CHECK ON SIDE LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • connections between Body Computer and steering column switch unit;
  • connections between ignition switch and Body Computer;
  • connections between Body Computer and bulbs;
  • bulb earth See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Continue to Step 4

Connections broken

Restore the connections See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

4

CHECK ON STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the steering column switch unit is working properly: in particular, the side lights switch:

Continue to Step 5

Stalk unit not working properly

Replace the stalk unit Op. 5540B32

5

CHECK ON IGNITION SWITCH

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the side lights come on when the ignition key is turned in the opposite direction

Continue to Step 6

Ignition switch faulty

Renew or replace the ignition switch Op. 5520A12

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Fault persistsOp. 5540B32
