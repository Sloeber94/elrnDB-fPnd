2127C CHECK FOR OIL LEAKS

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Smart gearbox

Visually inspect the pipes, pump, reservoir, hydraulic unit, actuators (engagement/selection, clutch) whilst 50 gear change cycles are carried out from 1st to 5th/R and from 5th/R to 1st (at a rate of 1 gear shift every 3-4 s).

Bleed the clutch using the Examiner Plus or Smart (active diagnosis mode).
