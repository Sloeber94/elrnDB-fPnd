Z095 DIAGNOSIS CANNOT BE STARTED ON THE AIRBAG CONTROL UNIT

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

To apply this procedure it is necessary to bear in mind that measurements cannot be taken on the Air Bag wiring.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The Air Bag wiring is a safety component. If there are problems with the cables or the connectors then the wiring should be replaced in one piece.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the connection for the diagnostic equipment battery terminals and diagnostic socket is correct.

See E8010 DIAGNOSTIC MULTIPLE CONNECTOR

Check that the connection at the control unit connector is correct

See E7030 AIRBAG

Move on to Step 2

Connections not correct

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Make the connections

See E7030 AIRBAG

2

ELECTRICAL POWER SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

With the ignition ON, check that the positive power supply is present up to the connector which connects the dashboard wiring with the Air Bag wiring

See E7030 AIRBAG

Move on to Step 3

No power supply positive

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Check the condition of the supply fuses and the continuity of the supply wiring.

In the case of a fault, renew it

3

EARTH CONNECTIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

With the ignition key OFF, check for the earth connection for the Air Bag wiring

Move on to Step 4

No earth connection detected

Restore the connection

See E7030 AIRBAG

4

EARTH CONNECTIONS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity between the control unit container and the vehicle earth

Move on to Step 5

Break in the connection detected

Restore the continuity by removing the paint or traces of oxide on the nuts/bolts fixing the control unit to the bracket and the latter to the vehicle bodywork

5

SERIAL LINE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the earth insulation and the continuity of the serial line between the diagnostic socket and the control unit

Move on to Step 6

Contact to earth or break in the connection detected

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Replace the wiring

6

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the continuity of the earth connection between the diagnostic socket and the earth

Move on to Step 7

Break in the connection detected

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Replace the wiring

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment and activate the dialogue

End of diagnosis

The diagnostic dialogue is not activated

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Replace the Air Bag control unit
