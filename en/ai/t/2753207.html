7040H30 front pillar trim - r r

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before removing and refitting the front pillar trim, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it; (2) wait for 2 minutes before continuing; (3) disconnect the negative battery terminal and insulate the cables; (4) wait for 5 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY FULES ( 5580C air bag system ).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Head Bag

Removing

Refitting

1. Partly release the front door housing seal.

Partly detach the front pillar trim from the fastenings and remove it.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the safety mounting.

3. Release the front pillar trim from the fastenings and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Head Bag

1. Partly release the front door housing seal.

2. Release the front pillar trim from the fastenings and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the front pillar trim in position and check that it is securely fitted.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Head Bag

Tighten the bolt fixing the safety mounting.

Place the front pillar trim in position and check that it is securely fitted.

Connect the battery terminals.
