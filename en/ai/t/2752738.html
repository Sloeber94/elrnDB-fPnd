1. Move the brake pedal to disengage the two retainers locking the brake servo pushrod, then release the pushrod from the brake pedal.

After each dismantling operation the retaining device must always be replaced even if it appears to be intact.

2. Undo the nuts securing the brake assembly to the brake servo.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the front brake pipe connectors from the pump.

2. Undo the rear brake connectors from the braking compensators.

3. Disconnect the vacuum intake pipe from the single-acting valve on the brake servo.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the brake servo/brake pump assembly from the engine compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the brake servo/brake pump assembly in the engine compartment.

Connect the vacuum pipe to the connection on the single-acting valve on the brake servo.

Tighten the front brake pipe connectors to torque.
