Operational description

The command for switching on the fog lights is given by the special switch in the control panel H090.

The body computer node M001 receives a negative signal (reference earth C020 connector B) at pin 11 connector O.

Having received the signal, the fog lights are switched on:

  • from pin 16 connector D sends a negative signal to the coil for relay T14 with an ignition-operated supply from the line protected by fuse F31;
  • the relay closes its internal contact and provides a direct battery power supply from pin 71 connector D of B001 to the fog lights incorporated in the front light clusters F010 and F011.

only if the side lights on signal is received at pin 7 connector P

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

.

Earth C020 is secured to the passenger compartment floor and is positioned near the right front wheel arch (footwell area).

The body computer communicates, from pins 35, 36 connector I, via the CAN, with the instrument panel E050 (for more details see wiring diagram E1050) controlling the switching on of the "fog lights" warning light.
