7045A10 l/h or r/h front seat (one) - r+r

To removed the fixed head restraint, carry out the operations illustrated for removing the backrest cover. The cover and the upholstery do not have to be separated.

Then proceed as described in the specific operation for the cover:

7045D31 Front seat BACKREST COVER, left or right - R.R. with seat removed
