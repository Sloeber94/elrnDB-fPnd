Dismantling

Reassembling

Support the engine in order to work on it in safety.

1. Working on the automatic tensioner, loosen the tension for the auxiliary shaft drive belt.

2. Remove the auxiliary drivebelt by prising off its drive pulleys.

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods

Reassembling

1. Use the automatic belt tensioner to slacken auxiliary drivebelt tension.

2. Remove the auxiliary drivebelt by prising off its drive pulleys.
