Before working on the airbag system, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) place the ignition key in the OFF position and extract it; (2) disconnect the battery terminals and insulate them; (3) wait for at least 10 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES (see assembly 5508C - AIR BAG SYSTEM)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Remove the compartment as described in the procedure:

5580C26 LOCK BARREL KEY/SWITCH for deactivating passenger 'AIR BAG - R.R.

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the lock striker.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the lock striker and tighten the fixing bolts.

Fit the compartment as described in the procedure:

5580C26 LOCK BARREL KEY/SWITCH for deactivating passenger 'AIR BAG - R.R.

Connect the battery terminals.

Check the operation of the Air Bag system using the Examiner or other diagnostic equipment.
