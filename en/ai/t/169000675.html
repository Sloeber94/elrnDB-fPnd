Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diagnostic tester will not allow diagnosis of the ABS control unit

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

DIAGNOSTIC CIRCUIT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the power supply for the diagnostic socket is correct

See E8010 DIAGNOSTIC MULTIPLE CONNECTOR

Also check the continuity of the K line

Check the operation of the Body Computer

Move on to Step 2 1

  • No diagnostic socket power supply
  • Continuity problems at K line
  • Body Computer defective
  • Restore diagnostic socket power supply
  • Renew the wiring, if necessary, replace the faulty components
  • Replace Body ComputerOp. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R R

2

ABS CONTROL UNIT POWER SUPPLY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the power supply to the ABS control unit is correct and the power supply to the diagnostic socket is correct

See E7020 ABS

Check that the fuse in the junction unit under the dashboard is still working properly

Move on to Step 3 1

  • No control unit power supply
  • Fuse blown
  • Renew the wiring, if necessary replace the ABS electrohydraulic control unitOp. 3340A12 ABS HYDRAULIC AND ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT
  • Search for and eliminate the problem that caused the fuse to blow, then renew the wiring

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check that it is possible to enter into the fault diagnosis for the ABS control unit

End of diagnosis
