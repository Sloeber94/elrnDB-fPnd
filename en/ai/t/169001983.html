Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

The flow of air sent by the fan motor in the passenger compartment using the manual speed control is not sufficient; it does not correspond to the speed set.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

Results of the tests

1

AIR DUCTS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the air ducts are intact and the vents are not blocked and are correctly positioned in the opening

Move on to Step 2

Air ducts or vents obstructed.

Restore the operation of the air ducts and the vents removing any material causing an obstruction.

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF SPEED SELECTOR

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fan is working at the maximum speed: if it only works at the maximum speed, the resistor should be replaced

Check whether the resistor thermal fuse is broken by measuring the electrical continuity at the thermal fuse securing points. .

Move on to Step 3

Failed operation of speed selector

Replace the ceramic resistor

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF FAN

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose: disconnect/reconnect the connection to the engine mounting. Check that it rotates at all speeds by activating them using the switch. If it does not rotate at the maximum speed, check the vehicle fuse. If the defect persists, before replacing it, provide a direct battery supply and check that it rotates at maximum speed.

Move on to Step 4

Electrical connections loose

Renew the electrical connections

Fan not working

Replace the fan

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning

The flow of air sent by the fan motor in the passenger compartment using the manual speed control is not sufficient; it does not correspond to the speed set.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Air Conditioning

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON OPERATION OF FAN BY CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

With the engine started, set the climate control system control unit in the AUTO position, adjust the temperature to 22 °C, position the recirculation to outside air intake and the distribution to the front position. Adjust the fan button (+ side) gradually increasing the speed set and then decreasing the speed using the (- side) button and check the effect on the variation of the air flow rate

End of diagnosis

There is no variation in the air flow rate

Move on to Step 2

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check:

  • that there are no errors at the passenger compartment fan
  • that there are no errors at the distribution actuator
  • that there are no errors linked to the fan (short circuit errors)

Move on to Step 3

Error at the passenger compartment fan.

Move on to Step 5

Error at the distribution actuator

Move on to Step 6

Short circuit error

Move on to Step 4

3

AIR DUCTS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the air ducts are intact and the vents are not blocked and are correctly positioned in the opening

Move on to Step 4

Air ducts or vents obstructed.

Restore the operation of the air ducts and the vents removing any material causing an obstruction.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF FAN

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose: disconnect/reconnect the connection to the engine mounting.

Repeat the checks using the Examiner and check whether the fan error reoccurs. Carry out the tests described in Test 5040CE in order

See Test 5040C AIR CONDITIONER BOX AND COMPONENTS

Move on to Step 5

Malfunction of the regulator built into the fan motor support

Replace the fan motor support

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose. Disconnect/reconnect the connection at the engine support. If it persists, before replacing the actuator, check the operation by manually activating the function that the actuator should carry out. With the engine running, switch on the fan at maximum speed. Place the flaps in the various distribution positions using the control/control unit (in manual mode). Check the air flow intake into the passenger compartment with your hand over the vents.

End of diagnosis

There is no air flow intake into the passenger compartment through the vents.

Replace the distribution actuator

Before refitting the replacement actutator, check whether the flaps inside the climate control unit may be broken or mechanically stuck
