Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV

Refitting

Removing

Thoroughly clean the area of the wheelarch where the side skirt will be bonded and remove any previous adhesive residues (where fastened to the same wheelarch).

1. Position the side skirt (1a) in its seat and secure using adhesive (1b) and studs (1c).

2. Tighten the fixing bolts.

If the side skirt removed previously is re-used, clean off any adhesive traces and apply a new adhesive with the same properties.
