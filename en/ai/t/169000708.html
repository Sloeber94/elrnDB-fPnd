Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1335 - Longitudinal acceleration sensor"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

This error is signalled if the sensor output voltage is below 0.3 V or above 4.7 V or if the yaw sensor supply voltage is more than 5.3 V or less than 4.7 V or if the value sent by the sensor is not plausible. This means that there is an open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the sensor or the wiring, that the sensor is mechanically damaged or that the reading circuit is damaged.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
