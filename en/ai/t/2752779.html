Refitting

Disconnect the battery negative terminal.

5580C18 air bag module - r+r

Before working on the Air Bag system, follow the safety instructions for working on the system very carefully (See 5580C - Air Bag System - Precautions).

Before removing the steering wheel, position the wheels as far as possible parallel to the car's longitudinal axis.
