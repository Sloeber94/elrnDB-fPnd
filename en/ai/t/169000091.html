Applies to: all engines; all production periods

To apply this procedure, the following must be borne in mind:1 - Measurements cannot be taken on the Air Bag wiring.2 - The system fault diagnosis is started leaving the vehicle charges connected.3 - The fault should be "Present" or "Present Intermittently" and not "Intermittent". If the fault is Intermittent then the Procedure is not applicable; proceed with an accurate check on the line where the fault was signalled.4 - Not all the simulation sensors are available (e.g. side impact sensors).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The Air Bag failure warning light is controlled directly through a dedicatec connection to the "Instrument Panel Node" from the Air Bag Control Unit.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The Air Bag wiring is a safety component. If there are problems with the cables or the connectors then the wiring should be replaced in one piece.
