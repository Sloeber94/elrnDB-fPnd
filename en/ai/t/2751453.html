Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Door lock

Description

The front ceiling light is the rocker type and contains a courtesy light.

The courtesy light is switched on/off according to the following logic:

  • to switch the courtesy light on permanently, press the light on the right hand side;
  • to switch the courtesy light off, press the light on the left hand side;
  • for automatic management by the body computer node, keep the courtesy light in the centre position.

The body computer node switches the courtesy light on automatically in the following circumstances:

  • when any of the doors (front or rear) is opened, for a maximum time of 3 minutes which is reactivated each time a door is opened;
  • when the ignition key is removed, for a maximum time of 10 seconds;
  • when the doors are unlocked (using the remote control or the driver/passenger door catch), for a maximum time of 15 minutes;
  • by turning the ignition key to the OFF position, for a maximum time of 10 seconds;
  • when the intertia switch is operated, for a maximum time of 15 minutes.

The body computer node switches off the courtesy light automatically in the following circumstances:

  • when the ignition key is turned to the POS position;
  • when the doors are locked (using the remote control or the driver's/passenger door catch)
  • when the inertia switch is reactivated.

The rear courtesy light switches on or off when the tailgate is opened or closed, respectively.
