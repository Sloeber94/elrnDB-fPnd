Applies to: engines: 1.1, 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Manual Air Conditioning

Description

The vehicle climate control system involves a duct/distributor unit with a heater, controlled via the knobs in the dashboard:

  • the left knob mechanically controls the mixture flap between hot and cold air;
  • the right knob, mechanically operated via a cable, controls the opening of the flaps which regulate the distribution of air flows;
  • the two central knobs are as follows: one with electrical control regulates the air ventilation at four different speeds, and the other mechanically regulates the 'recirculation' function of air in the interior compartment, via a flexible cable.

The operation of the different fan speeds is achieved through a resistive divider.

The control panel is lit up when the side lights are on.

The entire system is protected by specific fuses, housed in junction units in the engine compartment and under the instrument panel. A specific relay controls the switching on of the fan.

The fan can only be switched on when the ignition is on.

To obtain a cold interior compartment temperature, in addition to switching on the air conditioner, it is necessary to set the temperature knob to the blue area and regulate the air flow, and possibly set it to recirculation.
