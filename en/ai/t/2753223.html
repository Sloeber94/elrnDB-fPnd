Before removing and refitting the front seat with an airbag, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it; (2) disconnect the battery terminals and insulate them; (3) wait for at least 10 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY RULES (see assembly 5508C - AIR BAG SYSTEM)
