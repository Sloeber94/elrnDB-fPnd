Op. 3330D70 IDLER SHAFT ON BRAKE SERVO R R FOR RHD VERSIONS

1. Unscrew nut (1a) and remove pin (1b) from hole (1c) on the brake servo idler shaft mount.

2. Separate reaction link (2a) from the brake servo idler shaft mount (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Right hand drive

Refitting

Removing

Position the reaction link in the seat on the brake servo idler shaft mount.

Insert the retaining pin through the hole on the idler shaft mount, then tighten the nut.
