Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The diagnostic equipment signals one or more of the following errors: "C1042 - Right front magnetic wheel", C1037 - Left front magnetic wheel, C1052 - Right rear magnetic wheel, C1047 - Left rear magnetic wheel

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

This error is signalled if there is a lack of impulses for the speed signal for one of the wheels

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

FLYWHEEL TRACK CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the condition of the flywheel, removing the wheel if necessary.

Check that there are no metal shavings on the flywheel magnetic tracks.

Check that all the small magnets are correctly positioned.

Move on to Step 2

  • Metal shavings present
  • Front phonic wheel damaged
  • Small magnets missing or damaged
  • Rear [honic wheel damaged
  • Small magnets missing or damaged
  • Clean the flywheel track
  • Replace the hubOp. 4450C34 BEARING OF ONE FRONT WHEEL HUB, L/H OR R/H - REPLACE WITH PILLAR REMOVED
  • Replace the hubOp. 4450C34 BEARING OF ONE FRONT WHEEL HUB, L/H OR R/H - REPLACE WITH PILLAR REMOVED
  • Replace the hubOp. 4450D10 HUB OF ONE REAR WHEEL, L/H OR R/H - R+R WITH WHEEL REMOVED
  • Replace the hubOp. 4450D10 HUB OF ONE REAR WHEEL, L/H OR R/H - R+R WITH WHEEL REMOVED

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Delete the errors using the diagnostic equipment.

Carry out a road test

See Test 3340OA A.B.S. SYSTEM ACTUATION CHECK

to ensure that the control unit checks whether the errors are present.

To evaluate the correctness of the speed signal, follow the "Detecting flywheel signal" procedure.

Return to the workshop and check that the fault is no longer present using the Examiner.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Move on to Step 1
