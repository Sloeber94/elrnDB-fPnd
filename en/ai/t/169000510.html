The diagnostic equipment signals the following error: "C1110 Pump Motor"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

this error is signalled if the control unit detects and open circuit or short circuit (positive or earth) for the motor or the feedback, if the motor relay is broken, if the pump is stuck or if the operating circuit or the reading circuit is damaged

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
