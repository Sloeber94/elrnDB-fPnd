Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition key in the ON position, when the buttons on the doors are operated, the electric front windows do not work at all.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECKING WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) connected, check the CAN information:

  • for inactive control units;
  • control unit errors;
  • absence of control unit with error in CAN line

Continue to Step 4

Presence of faults

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Presence of faults

Continue to Step 2

2

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the fuses in the luggage compartment junction unit See E3050 ELECTRIC FRONT WINDOWS

Continue to Step 3

Fuse damaged

Replace fuse

3

CHECK ELECTRICAL SYSTEM

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check:

  • the condition of the connection between the luggage compartment junction unit and the driver's door node.
  • the condition of the connection between the luggage compartment junction unit and the passenger door node.

Continue to Step 4

Connections broken

Restore the connections See E3050 ELECTRIC FRONT WINDOWS

4

CHECK WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner Plus or Smart) to the diagnostic socket and check:

  • that there are no errors in the Driver's Door Node unit
  • that there are no errors in the Passenger Door node control unit

Continue to Step 5

Errors present in the Driver's Door Node and/or Passenger Door Node

Continue according to the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

5

SERVICES RELAY ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK (POWER CIRCUIT)

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the continuity of the connections between the services discharge relay and the driver's/passenger door node See E3050 ELECTRIC FRONT WINDOWS

Continue to Step 6

Connections broken

Restore the connections See E3050 ELECTRIC FRONT WINDOWS

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present
