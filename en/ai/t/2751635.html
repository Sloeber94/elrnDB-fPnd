Description

The BOSCH 8 ABS anti-lock braking system is used, with 4 ACTIVE SENSORS and 4 CHANNELS with integrated EBD (Electronic Brake Force Distribution).

The BOSCH 8 ABS system is the most advanced available today: it has a 12-solenoid hydraulic control unit.

Its active safety features are:

  • it ensures that each wheel close to locking has the highest possible braking, depending on the available grip;
  • It safeguards car control under limit situations when the wheels are about to lock.
  • maximum promptness of response;
  • ability to adapt automatically to the widest range of service conditions;
  • reduced weight of the control unit.

Like the passive sensors used on previous ABS, the ACTIVE SENSORS are responsible for measuring the speed at the wheels.

The basic characteristic of the active sensors is that the SIGNAL IS PROCESSED DIRECTLY BY THE SENSOR, instead of sending a square wave to the ABS control unit.

Main advantages:

  • intermediate intervention of the system;
  • ability to read speed signals close to zero (passive sensors cannot read speeds less than 2.75 km/h);
  • less sensitivity to external electromagnetic interference caused by electromagnetic fields.
  • they are not subject to interference generated by electrical road heating systems (used in some Northern European countries);

The electronic brakeforce distributor built into the ABS optimises car behaviour during braking due to the following characteristics:

  • improved brake force distribution;
  • regulation adapted to true wheel grip and pad efficiency conditions;
  • reduction in front brake temperature and brake servo thrust requirement;
  • continuous monitoring of system efficiency;
  • setting maintained in time.

The instrument panel also memorises and transmits on the B-CAN network the ON/OFF status of the 'ABS fault' and 'EBD fault' warning lights, and whether these lights are faulty.

The fault indication on the display of the instrument panel is specific for the individual fault; in other words, there is a fault message for the ABS system and one for the EBD system.
