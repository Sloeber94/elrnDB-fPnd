0010T21 coolant check density and top up if necessary

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Check that the coolant level is between the MIN and MAX marks on the expansion tank.

Carry out this check with the vehicle on a flat surface, several minutes after the engine has been switched off.

When the engine is hot, do not remove the tank cap; there is a danger of burn injury.

If necessary, top up the level by unscrewing the cap and adding coolant.

Fit the cap.

Start the vehicle and leave the engine to idle for a few minutes.

Turn off the engine and wait for a few minutes.

Recheck that the engine coolant level lies between the MIN and MAX notches on the expansion tank.
