Applies to: engines: 1.2 8v; all production periods; only with equipment: Heater and Smart gearbox and ESP

Functional description

The engine fan N011 receives the direct battery supply from the line protected by the fuse F06, operated by the relay T06. Depending on the parameters received from the coolant temperature sensor K036, the engine control unit M010, via a negative signal, energises the relay T06, activating the fan.

The engine management control unit M010 sends engine coolant temperature information, via the C-CAN See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES , to the body computer mode M001 (pin 11, 12, connector D). The body computer node sends this information, via the B-CAN, to the instrument panel E050, for the management of the temperature gauge inside the actual panel (for more details See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL ).
