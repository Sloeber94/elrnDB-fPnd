1040AA Check supply voltage and absorption current

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods

Check that the supply voltage to the pump on the pump connector is 13V.

Measure the pump absorption current by positioning the ammeter clip for the Examiner on the pump supply cable (on the fuse box or directly on the pump inlet cable [see electrical equipment]);

The current measured should be less than or equal to 4.6 Ampere.
