Operation

  • at the climate control node;

Check that the ambient temperature is at least 5 °C.

With the engine started and the compressor on (check that the snowflake symbol lights up), set the temperature to maximum cold LO and the distribution to the front position.

Using the diagnostic equipment check that the frost sensor gives a reading of above 3.5°C, also check that the instrument does not show any short circuit for the compressor circuit. In these conditions, check that the compressor comes on by opening the bonnet and taking a look.

Operation

Check for loose connections in the electrical wiring. Disconnect/reconnect the connection to the engine support. Check that it rotates at all speeds by activating them using the switch. If it does not rotate at the maximum speed, check the vehicle fuse. If the defect persists, before replacing it, provide a direct battery supply and check that it rotates at maximum speed.

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF CERAMIC RESISTOR

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fan is working at the maximum speed: if it only works at the maximum speed, the resistor should be replaced. Check whether the resistor thermal fuse is broken by measuring the electrical continuity at the thermal fuse securing points

End of diagnosis

Failed operation of ceramic resistor

Replacement of ceramic resistor

Problems encountered

Operation

With the engine started, set the climate control system control unit in the AUTO position, adjust the temperature to 22 °C, position the recirculation to outside air intake and the distribution to the front position. Adjust the fan button (+ side) gradually increasing the speed set and then decreasing the speed using the (- side) button and check the effect on the variation of the air flow rate
