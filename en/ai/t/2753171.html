The bead of sealant for the windscreen can be cut using either harmonic wire or the vibrating knife; both methods are described below.

To cut the bead using harmonic wire, proceed as follows.

1. Cut a section of harmonic wire about 50 cm long then, using pliers, insert one end (1a) of the harmonic wire through the bead of sealant (1b) which secures the window (1c).

2. Fix one end of the harmonic wire to the special retaining tool (2a), inside the vehicle, and the other end to the traction handle (2b) outside the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. The operator inside the vehicle should keep the end of the tool (1a) pressed against the bead of sealant (1b) about 30 cm away from the point where the wire passes, whilst the other operator, outside the vehicle, should pull the handle (1c), following the surround of the windscreen.

Repeat the operation in sections of about 30 cm, until the whole of the bead of sealant is cut.

2. Break the rearscreen side centering pins using a vibrating knife fitted with a suitable blade.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

To cut the bead using the vibrating knife, proceed as described below.

Open the tailgate.

Using the vibrating knife with a suitable blade to cut the bead of sealant and the centering pins.

1. Fit the suction handles to the rear windscreen and, with the assistance of a second operator, remove the windscreen and rest it on a supporting stand.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Using a suitable tool, cut and smooth off the bead of sealant in the rear window frame, so as to leave a thickness of between 0.25 and 1 mm, without scratching the paintwork.

The sealant left in the housing will act as a backing for the new bond.

2. Clean the rear window seating carefully with compressed air and degrease thoroughly with heptane.

If glueing takes place 60 minutes after cutting and smoothing the bead of sealant, treat the sealant surface with the specific activator and wait 15 minutes before fixing the rearscreen.

If this is not the case, use a new rear windscreen and proceed as described below.

1. Stick the new centering pins.

Remove the adhesive tape positioned previously on the edges of the bodywork and the light clusters.

2. Test fit to locate the window perfectly.

3. Once the rear window is centred, mark the reciprocal positions of the window and seating, with strips of adhesive tape.

4. Cut the strips of adhesive tape and remove the rear window.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
