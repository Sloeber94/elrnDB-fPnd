The cusomter complains of noise from the electric steering with the vehicle stationary, with the ignition off and/or whilst driving

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Results of the tests

1

CHECK ON TYPE OF NOISE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the system not activated (ignition OFF), check the type of noise reported

Continue to Step 2

Noise similar to rubbing plastic.

Continue to Step 5

2

CHECK ON TYPE OF NOISE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the system activated (ignition ON), check the type of noise reported

Continue to Step 8

Electric type noise (buzzing)

Continue to Step 4

Noise similar to a click

Continue to Step 6

Noise similar to reverse rotation of the steering wheel.

Continue to Step 7

Noise similar to high pitched creaking.

Continue to Step 3

Noise similar to a series of bangs during rotation.

Replace the EPS system Op. 4110D20 steering electrical control device - r r

Continue to Step 3

3

MECHANICAL CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the condition of the EPS electric motor rear bearing.

Continue to Step 8

The bearing is intact.

Replace the EPS system Op. 4110D20 steering electrical control device - r r

The bearing is damaged

Replace the system electric motor Op. 4110D30

4

CHECK ON INTENSITY OF NOISE

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the intensity of the noise (slight noise that cannot be heard on the passenger side is normal for an EPS system).

Continue to Step 5

The noise is loud

Replace EPS system electric motor Op. 4110D20 steering electrical control device - r r

5

INTERFERENCE CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

With the system not activated (ignition OFF), check:

  • interference of the steering wheel with the surrounding plastic parts.
  • interference of the steering wheel and/or driver's airbag with the surrounding parts.
  • interference of the steering column with the surrounding plastic parts.
  • interference between the lower forks connecting the steering box and the floor carpet.

Continue to Step 8

The steering wheel is in contact with the upper and/or lower trim.

Reposition the steering column trim.

Noise noticed from the steering wheel and/or one of its components

Restore the correct operation of the defective component.

The steering column and/on steering column switch unit is in contact with the upper and/or lower trim

Reposition the steering column switch unit and/or steering wheel cover

The fork is rubbing on the floor and/or carpet

Reposition the vehicle floor covering

Noise noticed from inside the electric steering

Replace the EPS system Op. 4110D20 steering electrical control device - r r

6

FORK TIGHTENING CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the lower fork nut is correctly tightened on the steering box pinion

Continue to Step 8

Tightening not correct

Restore the correct tightening

7

STEERING WHEEL OSCILLATION CHECK

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check the type of steering wheel oscillation

Continue to Step 8

The fault occurs when the steering wheel oscillation is slight

Replace the EPS system insulator (flexible coupling)

The fault occurs when the steering wheel movements are sudden, similar to emergency manoeuvres

Replace the EPS system intermediate shaft

8

Continue as instructed by the tester

All OK

Problems met

Operation

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the EPS system Op. 4110D20 steering electrical control device - r r
