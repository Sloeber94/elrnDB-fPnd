Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Undo the studs and remove the headlamp frame.

3. Undo the clip to disconnect the headlamp washer pipe.

4. Remove the headlamp frame.

Applies to: engines: 1.3 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: SUV

Refitting

Removing

1. Connect the headlamp washer pipe and secure with the clip.

2. Fit headlamp frame (2a), doing up studs (2b).

3. Tighten the fixing bolts.
